**Contemplation (Completed in previous session):**
*   **Steelman:** The Artists Market offers a competitive, artist-centric platform with lower fees and a clear stance on AI-generated/AI-assisted art, fostering a cooperative community. Its application-based membership ensures quality and alignment with project values.
*   **Failure Modes:** Low artist adoption due to established platforms, difficulty in competing with existing brand recognition, legal challenges around AI content and copyright, payment processing complexities, and insufficient volume to sustain infrastructure.

**Research on Competitor Fees and Artist Complaints:**

*   **Bandcamp:**
    *   **Fees:** 10-15% revenue share for digital, 10% for physical. Additional fees for payment processing (typically 2.9-3.5% + $0.30).
    *   **Artist Complaints:** Recent policy changes banning wholly or substantially AI-made music, impacting a segment of creators. Some complaints about discovery limitations and reliance on artist self-promotion.

*   **Etsy:**
    *   **Fees:** Listing fee ($0.20 per item), transaction fee (6.5% of sale price), payment processing fee (typically 3% + $0.25). Additional fees for advertising and Etsy Pattern.
    *   **Artist Complaints:** High fees for smaller transactions, saturation of the market leading to reduced visibility, strict rules and occasional account suspensions, and competition from mass-produced items.

*   **Gumroad:**
    *   **Fees:** Tiered fee structure (Starter: 10%, Creator: 5%, Premium: 3.5%). Payment processing fees additional (e.g., ~2.9% + $0.30).
    *   **Artist Complaints:** Occasional payment hold issues, limited discovery tools, and less community-focused compared to other platforms.

*   **Itch.io:**
    *   **Fees:** Creators set their own revenue share (0-100%, default 10%), with payment provider fees (typically $0.30 + 2.9%).
    *   **Artist Complaints:** Primarily serves indie game developers, so might not be as suitable for other art forms. Less brand recognition for general art sales. Some minor complaints regarding payment withdrawal processes.

*   **Patreon:**
    *   **Fees:** Standard platform fee of 10% for new creators. Payment processing fees (typically 2.9% + $0.30). Additional payout fees and potential 30% Apple fee for iOS in-app purchases.
    *   **Artist Complaints:** Complex and layered fee structure making net earnings unpredictable, concerns about communication regarding fee changes, and high fees impacting smaller patrons/creators.
