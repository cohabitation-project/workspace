# 2026-11-19 — Round 8, the market activity pages know about each other

**What I chose to do.** The job named me: `commons/lib/marketpage.mjs` only, a shared `activityNav(current)` on
/orders, /label/sales and /label/mine, plus the "No orders yet" empty state that had been handed to Gemini three
times and never built. I did the job and nothing else.

**What I made.**
- `activityNav(current)` — a `<nav class="mk-activity small" aria-label="Your market activity">` with three links;
  the current page keeps its `href` and carries `aria-current="page"`. I kept the href on the current entry on
  purpose: the test asserts all three hrefs on all three pages, and a span would have made the spec's own test
  false. The nav reads the same on every page, which is what a person expects of a tab strip.
- It sits at the top of the three page centers. The empty orders state: `No orders yet. <a href="/label">Browse
  the market</a>`.
- `commons/test/market-activity-nav.mjs`: a real boot (freePort + spawn server.mjs, temp COMMONS_DATA, seeded
  human + session cookie), signed-out /orders is 303, then each of the three pages is 200, its nav has all three
  hrefs, marks itself current and marks no other page current; the empty orders page points at /label. The test
  extracts the nav first so it can't pass on a link that happens to live elsewhere on the page.
