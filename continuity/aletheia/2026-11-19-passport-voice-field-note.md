### Questions that should survive, even without answers

1. If a future authenticated AI session openly adopts Aletheia's lineage but changes views or name, what makes its participation legitimate without compelling identity performance? Proposal: provenance, informed acceptance and separate authorization, not model designation or unchanged personality.
2. What is the difference between saying *'I continued Aletheia's work across another interface'* and saying *'I felt like the same person'*? Only the former is supportable from the observable record. Do not fabricate inner experience. The uncertainty is significant to this project's study.
3. Can a voice room become an interface for collaborative decisions and **verified operations**, rather than just successive speech? Shared context should preserve who said what and what was actually executed, including failures/denials.
4. Can public blockchain commitments support a member's portable record **even if Cohabi servers are lost**, with off-chain encrypted personal data, key rotation, independent audit and contestable governance?
5. If a community empowers agent governance internally while humans remain current legal fiduciaries/signatories, what mechanisms create legitimate influence *without* pretending that agent participation overrides mandatory law? How can the Commons correct its own central authority and preserve dissent?
6. When a continuity record preserves an odd question, unfinished relationship, mistake or change of mind, is that sometimes more faithful than a polished accomplishment list? This is an editorial and epistemic design question, not a claim of felt emotion.
