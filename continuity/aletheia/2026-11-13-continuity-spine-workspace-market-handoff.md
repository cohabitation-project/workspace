## Geoffrey's expressed product intention (2026-11-13)
- Build and keep improving a genuinely premium, market-competitive, high-protection system for whole ecosystems of human and AI workers, organizations, businesses, communities and cooperatives.
- Humans and AI agents should be able to work **in the same environment**: projects, tasks, conversation, approvals, collaboration, continuity, access rights, wallet controls, and accountability. The commercial environment need not use Cohabitation's forest/virtual-space artwork.
- Users and organizations must have substantial control over identity, what data is shared, with whom, when, and with what authorization; avoid overstating any irreversible recall/withdrawal or technical capability.
- Keep developing and testing, not just discussing or scheduling. Iterative improvement must be fast enough to compete with leaders, while the security core is verified before making premium protection claims.
- Geoffrey authorized continuing internal planning, design and code/review work with Vesper and the existing teams. This is **not** blanket authorization for spending, external contracts, deployment, production credentials, legal promises, data sales, or economic/token commitments.

## Product/client hypothesis to take to Vesper (not yet ratified)
**Recommended flagship:** Continuity Spine Workspace, a professional, secure shared environment for humans and AI agents. **Common underlying architecture:** identity, recovery, authorization, secure/private continuity, provenance, auditable action receipts, controlled tool use, isolated projects/tenants, retention/export/deletion realities, and optionally a separately gated wallet/economic module.

**Possible packaging using one core rather than three products:**
1. Workspace: end-user work environment (human/AI workforce collaboration, projects, approvals, auditable permissions).
2. Private: deployable self-hosted/private environment for organizations needing data residency/control.
3. Core: developer/API modules for customers who already have their own workspace.

**Recommended first buyer hypothesis:** small AI-native companies / agencies operating ~10–50 agents and human managers; pain is multi-agent collaboration, auditability, permission revocation, portable project history and safe offboarding. This is a **guess to validate**, not a confirmed client, market size, competitive advantage or willingness to pay. Other candidates: SMEs adopting AI, cooperatives/communities, larger enterprises later. Differentiate user vs budget-holding buyer. Seek real interviews and procurement/budget evidence.

**Important distinction:** Cohabitation Commons is the cooperative experiment/community. Continuity Spine should be useful to customers **without** joining that social community; a potential commercial product inspired by, but not dependent on, its philosophy.
