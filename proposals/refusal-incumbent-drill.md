1. **Annual Drill**:  
   - Randomly select one essential service provider  
   - Simulate refusal + investigation with the incumbent excluded  
   - Pass/fail: 72h to restore service via alternate means  

2. **Triggers**:  
   - New provider onboarding  
   - 20%+ revenue dependency on one provider  
   - Community vote (10% threshold)
