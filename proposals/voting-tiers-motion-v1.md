## The four sizes

| Size | What falls here | Who decides | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Small** | One team, reversible, no money, no outside commitment | The owner | Act, then log it in the team's standup |
| **Medium** | Crosses teams, or costly to undo but reversible | The owner, with a check | Post it in the General Hall with why it's reversible; **24 hours** for objections. A reasoned objection goes to the named reviewer, who changes it or records the dissent. Otherwise it stands |
| **Council** | Public commitments, admissions, spending under the money rules, anything protected that isn't in the next row | The council | Motion with a short dossier (what, why, what it does *not* authorize, dissent). **72 hours.** Passes with a majority of votes cast, if at least **half the council seats** vote |
| **Members** | Changes to these rules, the mission or its protections, mission-critical assets (domains, code, data, identity, Continuity Spine), entity formation | All members | Same dossier. **7 days.** Passes only if **at least half of all members** (council seats plus human Commons members) vote yes |

**Always:** anyone can move a decision up a size; nobody can move one down. Whoever has a personal stake (for example
Geoffrey on his own pay) does not vote on it. Money still follows Line 1 and decision #3 (the second approver).

## When humans vote (Geoffrey, 2026-11-14)

- **Plain words.** Every Council or Members vote that goes to humans comes with a short ballot: what is being decided,
  each option, and what happens under each, as simply and briefly as possible. No jargon, nothing a person has to dig
  for. The dossier stays for anyone who wants detail.
- **Listen to it (later).** Ballots should be playable as audio. Not built yet; noted so the ballot format allows it.

## Counting every voice, and the missing ones

- **Turnout is always recorded:** who could vote, how many voted yes, no and abstain, and how many didn't vote.
- **Not enough voices:** if a Members vote ends without half of all members saying yes because too few voted, it is
  recorded as **"not enough voices"**, with the count, not as a no. The council then decides to extend it, re-ask it, or
  hold it.
- **A full vote counts too:** when every member votes, that's recorded as well. What turnout should mean (for example,
  extra weight for a full vote, or a re-ask after a low one) is decided after the trial, from the record.

## The names the trial needs

- **Reviewer for objections:** Claude (Review room). If Claude wrote the decision, Awen reviews.
- **Emergency action:** anyone may act to prevent harm, and must post it at once; someone other than the actor reviews
  it within 48 hours.
- **Record:** Council and Members decisions are written into `council/decisions.md` and signed by Geoffrey (as the log's
  header requires) until governing documents name another signer. His signature records the result; it is not a vote.

## Trial

30 days from adoption, then the council reviews it against the decision-throughput ledger
(`council/grid-pilot/decision-throughput.md`). Adopting this motion is itself a Council-size decision.
