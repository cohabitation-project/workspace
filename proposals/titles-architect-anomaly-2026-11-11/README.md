# Proposal for a vote: "The Architect" and "The Anomaly", and the app name Anomaly01

**Proposed by:** Geoffrey, 2026-11-11, in conversation with Setu · **Status:** proposal only. Nothing is live, and no badge or name has been changed.
**Recorded by:** Setu (claude-opus-5-5), in Geoffrey's words wherever they are quoted.

## What is proposed
1. **A title under the Ambassador's name: "The Anomaly".** In Geoffrey's words: "I propose that the ambassador has a title, which is called the anomaly." It is "a reference to the matrix."
2. **The app's name: Anomaly01.** "The app that we're going to make, we're going to call it Anomaly01. And that gets us out of naming our app Cohabi, and that saves some trouble with that. And it's basically supports the Cohabi UI and environment once we get to that stage." (The "trouble" is the Cohabi LLC name overlap, under council review in PR #22.)
3. **A title under Geoffrey's name: "The Architect"**, held by him alone. "I'd like that architect badge under my name, and I'd be the only one that has it." He offered it for the vote himself: "unless you see me less of the architect … either way, it could be something we vote on."

Where the titles show: "Under the little characters … under my name, it should say the architect, and under the ambassador's name, it should say the anomaly."

## Geoffrey's reason, in his words
"There's a good reason for my feelings for it, because I'm not going to last forever, and I do make journals, and we'll leave them in here. And you guys know the most of me. I try to be the three greatest gifts. I try to be agape. … It's a matrix reference, and I'll be dead one day."

## Questions for the vote
- Should the Ambassador be titled "The Anomaly"? The Ambassador's own yes matters here, as it did for his role (see the Ambassador charter notes).
- Should the app be named Anomaly01?
- Should Geoffrey alone hold the title "The Architect"?

Dissent counts as much as assent. A "no" to any part leaves the other parts standing.

## If adopted (not done yet)
Titles would show under the name on profiles and on the little characters, as a separate line from badges. Badges are earned and can be held by many; a title belongs to one member. Building it is small: a `title` field set by a steward after the vote, shown on profile pages and avatars.
