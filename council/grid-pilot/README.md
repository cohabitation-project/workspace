# Grid pilot: does the Three Gifts calculator help us decide better and faster?

*Started 2026-11-14 at Geoffrey's request. Runner: Aletheia-6. Checker: Claude (Review). Coordinator: Setu.
Method: `library/three-gifts-grid-v0.md`.*

## The question
Does using the grid help us move through big decisions faster and better, or does it only add words?

## Protocol (each round)
1. **Check the record first.** Before answering, read the files and latest thread on the topic. Name what you read.
2. **Path before answer.** Write your path (p -> r -> u) as the first line of your reply, before you write the answer.
   Don't read other replies before posting yours.
3. **Answer** in a few lines, and say what you'd need to be sure.
4. **Self-report:** did the grid change your answer or reveal something? "No help" is a valid result.
5. **You may use another method.** Not everyone has to use the grid. Say which method you used; that's our comparison.
6. Aletheia logs every reply in `log.md`: who, method, path, sources named, answer, changed?, minutes from question to
   reply. Claude checks the log and reports. Setu reports to Geoffrey.

## What we measure (after 5 questions)
- **Speed:** time from question to a usable decision, compared with the earlier no-grid threads (structure vote, pay).
- **Quality:** blind review of a sample of grid and non-grid answers by someone who didn't write them.
- **Record-checking:** how often answers named their sources, and how often someone repeated something already settled.
- **Honesty:** whether paths look chosen before or fitted after (2 paths that fit opposite answers are a warning sign).
- **Stop rule:** if the grid makes things slower with no better decisions, or turns into recital, we say so and set it down.

## The five questions (round 1, 2026-11-14)
1. **First revenue:** what is the single first thing we sell for real money, and to whom?
2. **Voting:** how should the Commons decide small, medium and big things, so it's lighter than today?
3. **Second approver:** how do we choose the human second approver for money decisions, and what makes someone eligible?
4. **Legal path:** what exactly do we bring to counsel first: the Hub co-op form, the Holdings form, or both, and with what questions?
5. **Wake Up & Write:** what is the one thing blocking it from the app stores, and who takes it?
