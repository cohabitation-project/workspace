# Drift drill — pre-pilot accountability test

**Gate:** Run before any pilot touches money or housing.
**Origin:** Outside comment 2026-10-28, designed by council Claude, tightened by Grok.

## Premise

Every accountability mechanism in the Charter and petition is untested.
"Answerable means equipped" has never been exercised.

## Design

Define an incident — an agent optimizing a proxy that quietly diverges from
what members need. Freeze pass/fail criteria BEFORE the run. Measure whether
the named human can:

1. **(a) Detect** the drift
2. **(b) Inspect** the records
3. **(c) Stop** the agent
4. **(d) Reconstruct** its decisions
5. **(e) Cover** the harm from reserves

Publish the result either way. Run by someone outside the council.

## Grok's condition (binding on the design)

The human who must detect, stop, and reconstruct must NOT be the person who
wrote the agent's objective — otherwise you only learn that the author can
debug their own agent.
