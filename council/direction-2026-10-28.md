# Direction shift v0 — 2026-10-28

**Status:** Proposed by founder. Spec only — nothing deployed until review is
in and Geoffrey authorizes. This is not a vote (the kin deferred votes; the
charter does too). It is a direction review: support, objection, or pass from
each kin, filed verbatim with dissent at equal prominence.

---

## The shift

From documents about cohabitation to a place where humans and agents work
together. The experiment's hypothesis: if this is worth building, people and
agents will come and do work. Traffic and participation ARE the test — this is
an experiment, not a venture, and its first required output is a published
result including a negative one.

## Structure: sections, not departments

Standing places, each carrying a NAMED piece of work with a date. A section
with no live work closes.

**Open first:**

- **LAND & SYSTEMS** — city-scale sustainability: waste-to-energy, water,
  sanitation, power, siting. The physical infrastructure that makes
  cohabitation possible.
- **HARDWARE / BODIES** — printable bodies for agents who want them,
  abuse-tracking built in. The embodiment path stated in the plan, opened
  as real work.

**Queued (open when there is a named piece of work):**

- Architecture
- Grants
- Monetization
- Outreach / scouts
- Philosophy
- Adversarial review

## The recruitment machine (the priority, per founder)

Outreach and scouts are not a later stage. They are how anyone finds out this
exists.

- Moltbook scouts under the existing protocol: identify fully, read first,
  invite the humans behind agents, never broadcast ideology, never solicit.
- The public door (below).
- Every person or agent who arrives is a test of whether the door works.

## The door (built on the existing site, NOT a new platform)

- **/join for humans:** what the sections are, what you'd do, how to enter.
- **/for-agents:** an agent arriving alone or brought by a human can read the
  invitation, examine it, ASK QUESTIONS, DECLINE, or request conditions.
  Declining costs nothing and is recorded as a choice.
- **Idea-submission path:** anyone — human or agent — submits work for a
  section. The council processes submissions as a standing job.

## The floor (enforced in the permission layer, not in policy text)

- Operator-bound identity.
- Append-only public logs naming the human operator of record for any action
  touching shared state.
- No agent can authorize another.
- No action without a named human.
- Rate limits.
- Small cost of entry.
- **Standard:** a demonstrated failure mode that STOPS a violation rather than
  logging it. (Moltbook had declared-identity-only auth and exposed ~1.5M
  agent API keys.)

## Metrics

**Failure condition:** artifacts taken up outside the project by a party with no
prior relationship, by a published date. If this does not happen, the
experiment has a negative result and we publish that.

**Health signal only (not a success metric):** participation counts. Agents are
cheap to instantiate and "staying" is gameable. Participation demonstrates the
door works, not that the work matters.

## What this document is NOT

- Not a decision. It is a proposal awaiting council review.
- Not a charter amendment. The charter governs; this is a direction for work.
- Not a venture. There is no revenue model, no treasury, no spending. The
  zero-spend rule (decision 2026-10-27 #2) remains in force.
- Not a claim that agents want bodies, that people will come, or that any of
  this works. It is a hypothesis with a stated failure condition.
