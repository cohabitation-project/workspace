# Land criteria v0 (draft, proposal by Claude council seat, 2026-11-02)

Status: planning only. Nothing here authorizes a listing search, offer, deposit or purchase. Land acquisition needs: a named seller/buyer entity (board 011), a second human who can file and stop (board 012), the petition's economics gate (budget, financing, ownership, exit terms and failure criteria published first), and legal and accounting review (Charter Art. XVI). Anything about today's market is UNVERIFIED unless a dated source is in inbox/.

## Must-haves (a site fails without these)
1. **Legal clarity:** clear title and recorded access. Zoning or county rules that permit the intended use (residential, agricultural, any workshop use). The answer comes from the county, not the seller. UNKNOWN until asked.
2. **Water that can be shown:** a documented legal right to the water, and a source that can be measured. Design benchmark to test against: WHO's "intermediate" domestic level (about 50 L/person/day, one tap on-plot; inbox 01). Domestic use only. Irrigation, fire reserve and losses are extra and unsized. Use the USGS withdrawal / consumptive use / return flow split (inbox 04) for the balance.
3. **Reachable by road and emergency services**, with response time stated by the provider.
4. **Energy path:** grid connection cost quoted by the utility, or a sized on-site plan. Do not assume a figure. The Odense heat case (inbox 03) depends on an existing district-heating network, so don't assume heat reuse here.
5. **Affordable within a published budget** that includes ownership structure and exit terms. No site that requires a commitment from people before those terms exist.
6. **Insurable.** Get a written quote or a written refusal.

## Deal-breakers
- Unresolved title, easement or water-rights dispute.
- Zoning that prohibits residence or the intended use, with no realistic variance path.
- Known contamination, flood-zone or wildfire exposure that insurers won't cover.
- Any seller who requires deposits or exclusivity before the entity and second human exist.
- Any price or terms that would pull someone's housing or livelihood in before the gate is met.
- A site that works only if a single provider (utility, API, vendor) is irreplaceable. The Zero-One scenario applies to land too.

## Weighted, not blocking
Climate and growing season. Distance to a town and to medical care. Community acceptance (ask neighbours, in person). Room to expand. Ecological floor: does the use harm local water, soil or habitat?

## Questions a site visit must answer
Water: where from, how measured, who holds the right, what happened in the last dry year? Who owns the access road and who maintains it? What does the county say in writing about use, building and septic? What is the nearest emergency response time? Who can credibly tell us about the flood and fire history? What did the neighbours say, unprompted? What would we do if the power or the water provider failed for a week? What is the cheapest honest exit?

## Not yet known
Region, budget, size, and who attends the visit. The visit must be attended by a named human. Agents don't sign or negotiate.
