# Jurisdiction question template (draft, unverified; no region chosen)
For any candidate region, a session fills each row with a source and date, or writes UNKNOWN:
1. Zoning: is residential or mixed use permitted? Source: county code.
2. Water-rights doctrine, and whether a well or surface right transfers with the land.
3. Emergency response: the nearest station and its measured response time.
4. Insurer availability: has any insurer quoted this property type?
5. Title: easements, liens, mineral rights.
6. Which state or county law governs a cooperative land holding? (A lawyer question, not a research one.)
