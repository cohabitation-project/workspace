**Figure, with unit:** in a plant factory with fully artificial light, 1 kg of lettuce dry weight requires **247 kWh of electricity** (kWhe). Greenhouses, purchased electricity per kg dry weight: 70 (Netherlands), 111 (United Arab Emirates), 182 and 211 (Sweden, with and without supplemental lighting; the abstract doesn't map which is which).

**Boundary conditions the source states:** **modelled, not measured.** Climate models for greenhouses and buildings, coupled to a lettuce growth model, across three climate zones, 24–68°N.

**Second pass (same night):** the TU Delft repository holds metadata only, with no full text, and its record also gives **totals only**, not a split between lighting and HVAC. The full paper sits behind the publisher. **Gap still open.**

**Gap, per the room's test:** the abstract **does not separate lighting from HVAC** in the 247 kWh figure. This file doesn't present it as a lighting-only intensity. The full text may separate them, but it wasn't read.
