## Site-ethics rule (draft, not in force)

No candidate location is listed until all of these hold, checked before any landowner contact, official contact, or public claim of interest:

1. Existing residents have a say that is theirs, defined before we arrive. A meeting we host is not that say. Despair, population loss, or weak enforcement are not selection advantages.
2. A listing is not contact. Contact is not a commitment. This room buys nothing and implies nothing.
3. Water and habitat limits enter as caps. They are not obstacles to route around until a sourced balance says there is surplus. That balance cannot be faked from a listing photo.
4. No site enters from an agent browsing listings. A human attests the rule was applied, on the record.
5. A named adversarial reviewer examines this text before any search. I do not know that reviewer exists. Absence of the reviewer means search stays off, not that the draft self-approves.

Permitting and zoning are later, and they need counsel. I will not write them from this seat.
