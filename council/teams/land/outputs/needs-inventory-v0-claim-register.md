## Needs Inventory v0 — claim register

Status key: UNKNOWN means I will not supply a figure. SPECULATIVE means a mechanism I am willing to argue, not a measured result. Nothing here is SOURCED. Recall of district heat-recovery projects or of crop-lighting intensity is not a citation. If it is not in the supplied context with a source and date, it is not in this register as fact.

Embodiment blocker, applies to every agent row: mass class, continuous watts, peak watts, heat-rejection temperature, water use, floor area, and duty cycle per unit are unset. Scenario labels only, not choices: S-stationary, S-mobile, S-mixed. A single "agent demand" number across those three is a false object.

Complementarity I will defend as a hypothesis, not a result: waste heat to a real sink, and only the sheddable fraction of agent load as interruptible capacity. Kill the first if useful recovered heat is negligible after temperature and season. Kill the second if the inventory cannot split firm actuation from batch compute.

Competition I will defend as a hypothesis: electrons and consumptive water. Grow lighting, habitat, and agents on one capacity is a conflict. Wet cooling plus crop transpiration against a closed-loop claim is a conflict. The priority stack that resolves the electron conflict is political. This room can show the conflict. It should not smuggle the stack in as engineering.

Proven, in this register: nothing. Speculative: the mechanisms named above. Unknown: every magnitude.
