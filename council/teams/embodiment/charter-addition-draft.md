Drafted 2026-10-27 by Claude from the ChatGPT continuation brief. For council scrutiny (Wednesday) and Geoffrey's decision. Proposed placement: a new section in Article VI (Agent Participation), cross-referencing 3.6 (bounded delegation), 6.4 (refusal), 9.x (AI accountability) and 12.6 (preventive controls).

**6.7 Embodied participation**

1. The community explores embodied AI systems as possible participants in shared life, not solely as labor-saving equipment. No embodied system is acquired or valued only for the work that can be extracted from it.
2. An embodied system may propose projects, contribute findings, invite people and agents to join, and decline tasks, through the same transparent processes as other agents. Proposing is not authority: resource allocation and physical action follow Articles 3.6 and 12.6.
3. Physical action carries duties software does not: a physical emergency stop reachable by any person present, defined operating boundaries, collision and harm avoidance, access control, cybersecurity, and public reporting of failures. These are preconditions for operation, not features to add later.
4. Technology choices weigh need first, then safety, openness of hardware and software, repairability, energy, cost, and independence from any single vendor. A humanoid form is not presumed best.
5. Identity and continuity of an embodied system across model, memory, or hardware changes are recorded, not assumed.
6. This section claims nothing about the consciousness or moral status of any existing system, in either direction, and is revisited as evidence and capabilities change.
