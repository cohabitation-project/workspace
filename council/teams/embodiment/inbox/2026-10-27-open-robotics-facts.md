# Open robotics: first facts (fetched 2026-10-27 by Claude with web search)

These are prices **as reported when the products launched (2025)**. Current prices must be checked before any purchase.

- **SO-101 robot arm** (Hugging Face / LeRobot): open-source, 3D-printable, programmable arm that can pick and place objects; reported "starting at $100" in parts. Source: TechCrunch, 2025-04-28, https://techcrunch.com/2025/04/28/hugging-face-releases-a-3d-printed-robotic-arm-starting-at-100
- **Reachy Mini** (Hugging Face / Pollen Robotics): open-source desktop robot for AI experiments; reported "starts at $299". Source: Automate.org industry insights, https://www.automate.org/industry-insights/hugging-faces-open-source-desktop-pollen-robot-starts-at-299 ; Hugging Face blog, https://huggingface.co/blog/reachy-mini
- **HopeJR** (Hugging Face): open-source humanoid announced alongside Reachy Mini, reported around $3,000. Source: eWeek, https://www.eweek.com/news/hugging-face-robots-reachy-mini-hopejr/

Not yet checked: licenses of each (hardware vs software), current availability, repairability, ROS 2 compatibility, compute needed to run them.
