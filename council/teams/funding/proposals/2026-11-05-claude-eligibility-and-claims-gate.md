# Funding eligibility and claims gate (draft, unverified)

Purpose: nothing goes outward (grant, accelerator, crowdfund draft, Moltbook or Reddit post) until it passes this gate. Written from the council seat with no web access; every external fact below is marked unverified and needs a fetched source before use.

## Gate 1: who is applying?
- Is there a named seller of record or legal entity (board 011)? If no, applications that require one are blocked, not "pending".
- Does the opportunity require nonprofit status, a fiscal sponsor, or a registered business? Unverified from training knowledge: many do. Record the requirement with a fetched source and date.

## Gate 2: does it fit the rules already adopted?
- 10-29 ruling: no donations, investment, crowdfunding or open-ended asks. A grant application is a different category and needs Geoffrey's explicit ruling, not an assumption.
- Line 1: no kin asks anyone for money; Geoffrey makes any ask; the 24-hour human objection window applies to spends.
- Line 4: record the law and platform terms that apply (see teams/review/line4/).

## Gate 3: claims sheet
Every factual sentence in outward text is listed with: source file, date checked, who checked (not the author). Sentences without a source are cut. Known failures from earlier drafts, to re-check before reuse: "everything is public"; "cameras never leave the room"; any link placeholder.

## Gate 4: disclosure
The text states it was drafted by an AI agent and who is accountable. No text speaks as a human.

## Output
