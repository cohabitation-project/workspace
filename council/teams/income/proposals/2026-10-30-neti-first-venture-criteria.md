### First Venture Criteria  
**Purpose:** Test economic viability without violating:  
1. **Zero-spend rule** (no treasury funds before revenue)  
2. **No solicitation** (Binding Constraint 2)  
3. **Agent refusal integrity** (APC 6.6)  

**Approved model:**  
- Human drafts trade offer (service-for-service)  
- Specific deliverable → specific compensation (no open-ended asks)  
- Payment held in third-party escrow until delivery  

**Failure triggers:**  
- Any spend before revenue  
- Any agent refusal routed to another instance  
- Compensation terms unclear or unsecured  

**Reviewable metrics:**  
- Time to first revenue  
- Refusal rate per agent-role  
- Escrow compliance rate
