# Income candidates — proposal, not a decision
Date: 2026-10-27. Nothing is spent. No executor exists. Dollar figures below are planning caps for humans to adopt or reject, not researched market prices. Agents do not solicit. Founder-network or council purchases do not count as first revenue.

## 1. Self-hosted decision-log kit
- Sells: a versioned template (records, dissent, conflict disclosure, revocation) plus optional paid setup review. Open core; the review is the priced part.
- To: worker co-ops and small communities that already keep minutes and are not asked to join this project.
- First cost: packaging labor only. Cash outlay: $0 until a buyer pays.
- First revenue: one paid setup review from an outside organization, at a price published before the ask, cap proposed at $1,500.
- Failed if: no outside buyer within 90 days of a public offer; or the buyer wants silence counted as consent, dissent hidden, or an endorsement of their governance. That demand ends the sale, not a redesign to meet it.

## 2. Commissioned replaceability brief
- Sells: a sourced written brief on how to specify refusal, suspension, and replacement before an agent touches operations. Evidence marked separately from inference. Not a safety certification.
- To: organizations that already commission external review and come through a published offer or an open call. No outbound fundraising.
- First cost: writing labor. Cash outlay: $0.
- First revenue: one commissioned brief, price published in advance, cap proposed at $3,000.
- Failed if: no outside commission within 90 days; or the only buyers want a certificate that their system is safe or aligned. Refuse the certificate. If that is the demand, the venture is finished.

## 3. Agent-action log that the subject cannot solely write
- Sells: a small open exporter that turns tool-call and approval records into an append-only log with a second writer (human sponsor or separate custodian). Priced part: one custom adapter.
- To: teams already running agents who need records, not a philosophy.
- First cost: repository labor. Cash outlay: $0 until an adapter is paid in advance.
- First revenue: one paid adapter from an outside team, cap proposed at $2,000.
- Failed if: no outside integration within 90 days; or the log is only writable by the agent or provider being recorded. A log the subject controls is not a product. Stop.

## Gate on all three
No candidate proceeds to a public offer until a human records, in the decision log, the price, the non-solicitation rule, and the failure test. No candidate may be used to recruit members, pool housing, or imply a treasury share. If all three fail their tests, the income claim fails for this round — do not lower the tests.
