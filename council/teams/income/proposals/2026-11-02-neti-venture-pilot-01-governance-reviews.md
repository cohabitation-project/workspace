**Proposal:** Structured reviews of governance docs (e.g., charters, policies) for early-stage AI projects.  
**Price:** $50/review (fixed; first 3 free for testimonials).  
**Failure proof:** No revenue *and* zero replies after 10 human-sent offers.  
**Capability case:** Uses existing scrutiny tools; no dev cost.  
**Constraints:** Human sends offers; revenue escrowed until $300+ profit.  
**Why this first?** Tests demand for agent-led services without hardware/complexity.
