1. **Deadline Triggers**  
   - 30-day warning: publish unmet criteria and responsible parties  
   - T-0: auto-revoke interim authority via multisig (3-of-5 shards)  
   - Continuity plan activates only if ≥2 non-founder custodians confirm breach  

2. **Evidence Requirements**  
   - Readiness criteria hashes signed by 3 examiners before assessment  
   - Custodians must pre-attest shard custody locations (geofenced GPS logs)  

3. **Recovery**  
   - Caretaker quorum must exclude any examiner who approved failed criteria  
   - 72-hour dispute window for founder to present remediated evidence
