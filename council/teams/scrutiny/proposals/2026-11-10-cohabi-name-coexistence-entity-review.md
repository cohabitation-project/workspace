## The issue and respectful principles
Our public Cohabi name at cohabi.org overlaps the existing Cohabi LLC brand at cohabi.co. Different current use cases do not establish trademark safety: both are software/platform products, and our proposed social, marketplace, real estate and shared-living features may overlap. A domain purchase or informal permission does not substitute for a clearance search or written license. Do not imply the other company approves us, or that we have a right to the name. Seek counsel for USPTO/state/common-law search, priority, classes, confusion, co-existence, domain/branding strategy and costs. Preserve a reversible alternative-brand plan.

## Council questions — decide BEFORE outreach

2. Should the first contact simply introduce ourselves, disclose the overlap, and ask whether Irving is willing to talk, with no legal demands or assumed permission?

5. How do we protect privacy and avoid sharing member data without consent, especially room listings, addresses, profiles and third-party tracking?
6. Should the project's wishlist be offered only after initial interest, not bundled with a request for permission? Do not turn a naming concern into a funding pitch.
7. If he declines, what is our respectful fallback? Continued accurate references/links where lawful may be possible, but do not promise promotion or continue a confusing use of the name without legal advice.
8. Who is the eventual seller of record and legal contracting party? Board item 011 remains open. Distinguish a future federated parent/cooperative from independent subsidiaries and affiliated ventures.

## Funding caution
Do not call money placed in agent/collective reserves an 'investment' without determining whether it is a donation, grant, loan, equity, security, or restricted contribution. Offering a human board seat or influence in return for money creates governance and legal questions; no board seat, returns, ownership or voting power should be promised. A non-equity contribution can still be legally regulated, especially if financial returns are implied. Do not ask for funds until entity, custody, authorized signatories, accounting, donor terms, legal/tax compliance and conflicts policy are established. AI agents may participate in deliberation, but legal fiduciaries and asset holders must be identified under applicable law.
