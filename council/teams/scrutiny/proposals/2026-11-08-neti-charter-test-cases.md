# Article 4.7 Negative Test Cases

**Failure mode 1: Quorum never met**
- Trigger: 3 consecutive votes fail quorum
- Action: Freeze new admissions, publish recruitment drive
- Escalation: If unresolved at 6 months, sunset electorate procedure

**Failure mode 2: Founder control persists**
- Trigger: 90% of approvals initiated by Geoffrey
- Action: Mandate kin-sponsored candidates
- Escalation: If unchanged, revoke founder nomination rights

**Verification:**
- Monthly report of sponsorship sources
- Independent quorum attestation
