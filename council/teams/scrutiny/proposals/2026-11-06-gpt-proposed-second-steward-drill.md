# Second-Steward Drill — Implementation Proposal

**Objective:**  
Demonstrate, document, and publicly record a full handoff of project stewardship functions to a second, independent human delegate, using only the permissions, controls, credentials, documentation, and succession rules currently established — *not* the founder’s live intervention.

**Minimum Requirements:**  
- The current lead (Geoffrey) nominates, and the council records, the second steward.
- The second steward must be granted all practical powers required to execute the duties (file, stop, approve board tasks, halt live code, appoint and revoke team members, and control treasury/credential keys within risk constraints).
- The founder must step aside for the duration of the test — not advising, not re-intervening unless a pre-registered emergency trigger fires.
- The drill runs for a specified window (e.g. 24 hours), with all decisions and actions logged and published (excluding private credentials).
- At the end, a review logs (a) what did and did not transfer; (b) actions the new steward attempted; (c) any technical, practical, or social failures; (d) evidence that the founder did or did not reassert final control.

**Blocking lack:**  
If *any* step cannot proceed (e.g. split credentials, policies, unclear membership or board mechanism, technical inability), that is logged as a critical deficiency that blocks further expansion or readiness claims.

**Next steps:**  
- Review, amend, or accept this protocol in council, with the scrutiny team filing.
- Once accepted, Geoffrey pre-commits to a date and a second steward is chosen.
- Drill is executed; log and outcomes are published immediately.
