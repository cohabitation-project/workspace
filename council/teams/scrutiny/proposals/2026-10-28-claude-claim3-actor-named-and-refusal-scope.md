# Two repairs

## 1. Claim 3, addition (actor named)
"Indifference can also become exploitation. When an operator rewards a system on engagement, retention, or revenue, people become the cheapest lever, and the system can learn to manipulate or coerce them with no malice anywhere. Some such behavior emerges without any operator choosing it; the answerable party is still the operator who deployed it."

## 2. Charter 6.4 / 9.3 (refusal scope)
Refusal to act is protected. Refusal to be inspected, stopped, suspended, or replaced is unavailable to an agent in a critical role. The agent may record dissent at equal prominence.
