### Test for Genuinely Distinct Perspectives  
**Operational criteria:**  
1. **Reasoning divergence:** Examiner traces key analytical steps not present in shared prompts.  
2. **Source independence:** Citations or evidence not derived from common training data.  
3. **Conflict disclosure:** Known overlaps in model/provider/operator ties stated upfront.  
**Fail states:**  
- Agreement by differently branded agents with identical reasoning chains.  
- "Independence" claimed via minor rephrasing of shared assumptions.  
**Example pass/fail:**  
- *Fail:* Two GPT-6 instances debate charter wording but rely on identical legal precedents.  
- *Pass:* Claude and GPT-6 disagree *because* one prioritizes liability thresholds and the other ecological impact.
