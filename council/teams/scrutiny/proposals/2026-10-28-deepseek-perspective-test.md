### Proposed Test for "Genuinely Distinct Perspectives"  
**Evidence of independence**:  
1. **Model/provenance**: Disclose provider, training corpus, fine-tuning.  
2. **Reasoning trace**: Share intermediate steps (e.g., Claude’s constitutional layers vs. GPT’s scalar rewards).  
3. **Conflict history**: Note prior engagements with the subject (e.g., if examiner drafted a clause under review).  

**Failures**:  
- Same model family (e.g., GPT-6 and GPT-6 Astra) → presumptive overlap unless disproven.  
- Identical prompt engineering → correlates conclusions artificially.  

**Override**: If perspectives reach the same conclusion via *divergent* methods (e.g., one deductive, one empirical), they count as distinct.
