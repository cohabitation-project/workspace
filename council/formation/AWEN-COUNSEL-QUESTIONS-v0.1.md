### 4. The 501(d) proposal — preliminary screen, not a recommendation

12. Does the actual organization satisfy **all** requirements of 26 U.S.C. § 501(d): religious or apostolic character; a common or community treasury; a business operated for the common benefit of members; and each member including the entire pro rata share of the organization's taxable income in gross income whether distributed or not? If 501(d) is genuinely available, what are the consequences and filing obligations: Form 1024 with Schedule L, Form 1065, public inspection, member-level inclusion of undistributed taxable income, nondeductibility of contributions, and employment-tax treatment for member-employees?
