## The decision, not another debate

**Structure.** Preserve the adopted 2026-11-14 direction in council/decisions.md #2: the Hub cooperative governs itself, Holdings serves it by contracts, and mission-critical assets cannot be disposed of on a simple majority. For counsel, favor the November 20 purpose-trust -> Anomaly 01 LLC / Commons -> separately governed cooperative Hub proposal, with a lawful LLC-first operating sequence. Do not assert a trust, LLC or Hub already exists or that the proposed 6/2/2 board votes are lawful. The alternative November 17 Hub-owns-LLC design remains recorded, not silently erased. Human legal signatories and independent challenger of Commons decisions remain essential.

**Three financial systems, not one speculative currency.** (1) USD card/Connect payments and creator payables; (2) separately named, purchased, USD-denominated FIRST-PARTY service credits, to be activated only after legal/processor underwriting with funded liability accounting; (3) separately accounted EARNED recognition coins and tips, no dollar claim, automatic voting rights, guaranteed cashout or shared peg. A prepaid $50 first-party pack can reduce repeated small-card fixed charges. It does NOT eliminate artist cash-out, stored-value rules, supplier obligation, card processing or refunds. Future purchased credits for creator goods and funded creator royalties is a different merchant-of-record design needing contracts/Stripe/counsel first. Free and paid members may access token utility under product rules; only lawful cooperative membership governs voting.

**Proposed test-pilot rates.** Standard seller platform commission 7% of product subtotal; qualified co-op seller 5% if a legally defined member benefit is in effect. BOTH rates are plus the actual payment-processing cost borne by the seller, itemized on the seller's order/receipt, no hidden processing subsidy by Cohabi. No listing fee. Suggested $5 CARD CART minimum, with grouping/bundling for inexpensive songs or assets. Never claim universal cheapest pricing. Bandcamp's current help lists 15% digital and 10% physical plus processing; Etsy U.S. public seller page shows 6.5% transaction fee, 20¢ listing and 3%+25¢ processing. The old 7%/3%, 10%/7%, and 10% break-even assumptions were PROPOSALS, not approved rules; this 7%/5% proposal supersedes Aletheia's unsafe previous recommendation but is NOT adopted or live.

**Important technical detail.** Current commons/lib/checkout.mjs uses Stripe Connect Express destination charges with transfer_data.destination and COMMONS_FEE_PERCENT default 0. For destination charges the platform pays card processing. To achieve seller-borne processing in substance, the platform must calculate/itemize and withhold its commission PLUS actual processing cost from transferred proceeds, with correct Stripe routing, refunds, payment method fees, payout costs and accounting tests. Merely changing COMMONS_FEE_PERCENT to 7 does NOT implement this plan, so do not do that. Depending on Stripe account pricing selection additional Connect active-account and payout fees exist.

## $10 transaction example, illustrative current US standard card fee 2.9% + 30¢
Standard: $10 buyer payment, $0.70 commission, estimated $0.59 processing, $8.71 seller net, $0.70 platform net revenue BEFORE support, Connect payout fees, refunds, disputes, tax, reserves and shared overhead.
Qualified member: $10 buyer payment, $0.50 commission, $0.59 processing, $8.91 seller net, $0.50 platform net BEFORE the same other costs.
At $5, processor ≈ $0.45; regular commission $0.35; member commission $0.25. The processor cost must be explicitly recovered or the platform loses money. These are examples, not actual Stripe account invoices.

## Operating economics: Marketplace revenue is ONE source, not the whole operating budget
The 1,200 dollars per YEAR in income break-even v0.4 EXCLUDES major AI agent and marketing costs; unusable as all-project budget. The previously discussed $30–40 per day AI costs would be $900–$1200 per 30 days, a SCENARIO not reconciled to /spend or hosting invoices. At 7% platform revenue and seller-paid card processing, $900–$1200 of overhead alone requires around $12,857–$17,143 of monthly GMV to cover BEFORE other costs. At a 6% blended commission it is $15,000–$20,000 monthly GMV. This is not a forecast. Early operations must be supported by separately priced real-margin services: Roll In Host SaaS, FireDamm web/automation services, paid platform tools and subscriptions where lawful, publishing/AI content services, plus marketplace commissions. Every service has its own measured inference, staff, vendor and support costs. No treasury fee subsidies disguised in discounts.

Proposed reserve rule for approval: retain 10% of NET platform contribution for reserve until 3 months' verified expenses exist; do not label this patronage or legal reserve obligation without review. Show actual all-provider spend, hosting, storage, support and variable COGS monthly, with conservative scenarios and cash runway.
