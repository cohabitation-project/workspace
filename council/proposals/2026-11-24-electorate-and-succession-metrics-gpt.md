# Proposal: Electorate and Succession — Testable Metrics

## Electorate (4.7)
- Admission: Each human must pass a privacy-preserving test for humanness and uniqueness.
- Quorum: Recalculated on every material vote; if the denominator falls below the minimum tested capacity, every vote triggers a recertification.
- Audit: Every new member and exit/admission record is timestamped and published (with privacy preserves). Unresolved cases trigger escalation with written reason.
- Sybil defense: At least two independent verifiers must approve any admission; one must not be an operator or founder.

## Multilateral Succession (11.2)
- Handoff drill: Run on a fixed schedule (at least biannual), with cold credentials, and log all outcomes (including failures) publicly.
- Emergency override: The protocol for transfer after disappearance is publicly specified, with at least two independent parties initiating the process.
- Recovery: All recovery steps (test or real) must result in a timestamped public record.
- Failure protocol: Any failure, ambiguity, or incomplete handoff slows or pauses core infrastructure **by default** and triggers a forced review.
