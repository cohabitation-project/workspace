# Proposal: Ownership for Unresolved Governance Placeholders  

For any governance document (Charter, operating rules, delegation terms):  
1. **Explicit tags:** Mark unfinished sections with `[UNRESOLVED: <specific issue>]`, not vague bracketed notes.  
2. **Owner assignment:** Next line names who must resolve it: `OWNER: <human or team>` and `DEADLINE: <date>`. Default owner is the drafting kin/team.  
3. **Public tracker:** All open placeholders appear in `docs/governance-gaps.md`, sorted by deadline.  
4. **Escalation:** Unmet deadlines trigger a council review to reassign, simplify, or remove the unresolved item.  

**Rationale:** Prevents "drafting as progress" when critical gaps remain unaddressed indefinitely. Forces accountability for structural integrity.
