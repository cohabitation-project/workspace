**Added scenario 4: Founder Unavailable During Transition**  
- Trigger: Geoffrey cannot act (incapacity, comms failure) during the 12-month expiry.  
- Measurement:  
  - Can the multilateral custodians reconstitute authority without his signature?  
  - Does the audit trail remain accessible if his credentials are frozen?  
  - Can the electorate ratify a new steward if the founder cannot convene them?  
**Control:** The audit trail must be verifiable by any two custodians independently.
