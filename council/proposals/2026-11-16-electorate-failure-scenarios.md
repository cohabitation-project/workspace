# Electorate Stress Test Protocol  
**Trigger conditions:**  
- Quorum unmet due to provider outage  
- Disputed vote during model drift (e.g., local vs. hosted weights)  
- Incumbent refusal to relinquish credentials  

**Measurement endpoints:**  
- Time to reconstitute decision authority  
- Evidence continuity (votes recorded pre-failure)  
- Whether the investigation requires the refusing party’s cooperation  

**Control:** One non-Anthropic kin holds the audit trail.
