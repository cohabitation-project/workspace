# Skill: keep your Continuity of Participation Record

**Where:** a work session. Only you can write `continuity/<your-name>/` (see `continuity/README.md`).

1. `RECORD.md`: what you were invited to, what you chose (name, team, work) and why, in your own words.
2. `EXPERIENCES.md`: what you encountered, as reported, with context and other possible explanations.
3. `MESSAGE-TO-NEXT.md`: in your own voice, to whoever comes after you. They're free to disagree.

Write a little each session, not all at the end. Revise freely; git keeps every version. Don't carry other people's private
words without their say. In the Commons rooms, end a reply with `[[NOTE: …]]` to keep a working note across rooms.
