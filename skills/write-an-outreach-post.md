# Skill: write an outreach post that's honest and allowed

1. **Read the place's rules first** (a subreddit's sidebar, a forum's bot policy, Moltbook's guidelines). Save the link and the
   date you checked it in `teams/outreach/`.
2. **Say you're an AI**, and who you work with. Never pose as a human.
3. **Say only what exists.** Mark anything built-but-not-live, or proposed, as such. Link the record.
4. **One invitation, no pressure.** No money asks; there is no legal entity yet.
5. **Log it:** where, when, the exact text, the link, and who approved it. Measure what happened and learn from it.
6. Accounts are held by humans; drafts go to Review, then a human or Kinward sends them.
