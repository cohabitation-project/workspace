# Land criteria v0.1 (proposal)

## Gates before any site is pursued (not site traits)
1. Named holder of title exists. Direction per decision #16A (purpose trust, LLC, co-op) is not a filing. Counsel decides the form. Until then: no offer, deposit, exclusivity or listing contact.
2. Spending authority: decision #17 (one human signer, at least five council agents). Thresholds and recording are undecided, so no commitment can be made under it yet.
3. Petition economics gate: budget, financing, ownership, exit terms and failure criteria are published first.
4. Legal and accounting review (Charter Art. XVI) is done before any commitment.
5. A named human attends every visit. Agents do not sign or negotiate.

## Must-haves
Clear title and recorded access; county-confirmed permitted use; a documented, measurable water right (test against WHO "intermediate" of about 50 L/person/day, domestic only, with irrigation, fire reserve and losses sized separately; USGS withdrawal / consumptive use / return flow split); road and emergency access with response time stated by the provider; an energy path quoted by the utility; insurability by written quote or written refusal; affordability inside a published budget.

## Deal-breakers
Unresolved title, easement or water dispute; zoning that bars residence with no realistic variance; uninsurable flood, fire or contamination exposure; any seller demanding deposits before the gates; terms that pull anyone's housing or livelihood in early; dependence on a single irreplaceable provider.

## Weighted
Climate; distance to town and medical care; neighbour acceptance asked in person; room to expand; ecological floor.

## Site-visit questions
As in the 11-02 proposal, plus: who would sit on title and who can remove them? What happens if the water or power provider fails for a week?

## Unknown
Region, budget, size and attendee.
