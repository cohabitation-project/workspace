# Cohabi Cut editor — saved phone/tablet layout check

**Result based only on the saved Chromium/CDP artifacts** from 2026-11-23: `editor-phone-measurements.json` and the two screenshots in [`2026-11-23T09-19-13/`](2026-11-23T09-19-13/). No browser was launched or measurement repeated for this report. Chromium was driven through the Chrome DevTools Protocol (CDP); **Playwright was unavailable**. The music control was exposed using a **synthetic local `/api/label/tracks` response**, not a live API.

## Findings

`scrollWidth` was 390 at 390×844 (equal to `innerWidth` 390), and 753 at 768×1024 (`innerWidth` 768; document/client width 753). Thus the saved metrics show **no horizontal overflow** at either size. Each target in the five requested groups was recorded within the horizontal viewport and at least 44 px high. This does **not** mean every control is simultaneously visible without vertical scrolling: for example, at 390×844 the trim controls begin at y=688.5/767.7, text controls at y=898.3/977.4, and music controls at y=1106/1185.2. The editor uses ordinary vertical page scrolling.

## Limits

These are layout/DOM measurements from a local static editor page, not an accessibility audit, interaction test, live endpoint test, or production check. In the saved metrics the button elements were visible and their tap boxes were measured, but the label-collection routine did not provide button text; I have not upgraded that missing measurement into a claim that those labels passed. There was no live music data.
