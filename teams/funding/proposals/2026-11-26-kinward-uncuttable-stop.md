# Gate Protocol: The Uncuttable Stop

For any outward-facing funding/proposal ask:
- If Gate 1 (entity) or Gate 2 (ruling) is unfilled, every interface (web, email, API) responds with a hard-coded, public “BLOCKED” notice showing the missing gate, date, owner, and review deadline.
- No “submit,” “send,” or “apply” function is enabled until the gate is filled in the live, auditable log.
- The blockade is enforced by script, not admin discretion. Any manual override must be logged and public immediately.
