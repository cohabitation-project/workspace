# Cohabi Marketplace Fees

This page explains how Cohabi’s marketplace fees work, with examples.

## What a Seller Keeps

Sellers currently keep 100% of their sales on the Cohabi Marketplace for most goods and creative works. Our cut is 0% today and remains so until a decision is made otherwise for these categories. While Cohabi bears hypothetical card processing costs for these illustrative examples (if transactions were real), sellers still receive 100% of the transaction value under the current 0% fee.

**Important: All transactions are currently for test money only.**

**Future Services & Fees:** For specific marketplace services, such as book publishing (which will be a dedicated Cohabi marketplace service), a fee schedule will apply. This schedule is currently under proposal and will be detailed here once decided. Music distribution via third-party platforms like DistroKid has been decided against for Cohabi's direct offerings. The Lulu print-on-demand pipeline is in active development, and its specific fee implications will be detailed upon launch, with the maker bearing the Printful base cost.

## Worked Examples (Current 0% Fee for Goods & Creative Works)

These examples illustrate what a seller receives today for goods and creative works, based on our current 0% fee. For these **illustrative examples**, we show hypothetical card processing costs that Cohabi **would bear if transactions were real**. With a 0% fee, sellers currently keep 100% of the **test money** transaction value.

### $5 Sale
For a $5.00 sale:
*   **Buyer pays:** $5.00
*   **Cohabi gross fee:** $0.00
*   **Direct seller share:** $5.00
*   *Illustrative example of hypothetical card cost to Cohabi: -$0.445*

### $20 Sale

### $100 Sale

## Status of Fee Proposals

All percentages for seller fees beyond the current 0% for goods and creative works are **proposals, not decided** as of 2026-11-28. The proposed services fee schedule for areas like book publishing is under review. The figures shown in the "Royalty and fee tables v0.2" document are calculated illustrations from these proposals, not observed sales or adopted rates. The assumed card processing costs are also illustrative and not current processor price quotes.
