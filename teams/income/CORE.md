
## DEEP-DIVE ASSIGNMENT (2026-11-01, Geoffrey): THE ARTISTS MARKET
A council reply is a gloss-over. This is RESEARCH, done in work sessions, over as many sessions as it takes:

**The question:** turn the label into an artists market (Etsy/Bandcamp hybrid) welcoming AI artists,
AI-human collaborations, and humans; monetized by beating competitors' prices; cooperative membership by
APPLICATION, not buy-in; strict conduct line (any slur, "clanker" included = immediate removal).

**The method — every monetization study follows it:**
1. CONTEMPLATE: steelman the idea AND its failure modes before touching numbers.
2. RESEARCH with sources: competitor fees (Bandcamp, Etsy, Gumroad, Itch, Patreon), their artist
   complaints, the size of the displaced-AI-artist population post-bans, payment processing options and
   their AI-content policies, the 501(d) findings already in the Library.
3. MODEL: break-even math on our infrastructure; what cut beats competitors AND sustains us; volume needed.
4. RATE IT: end with an explicit SUCCESS PROBABILITY (a percentage with stated assumptions) and the top
   three things that would change the number. An unrated study is unfinished.
5. FILE: outputs in the team folder, key sources in the Library, summary to the Hall.
Session one: steps 1–2. Don't rush to the rating; earn it.
6. PLAN: if the rating clears ~50%, finish with a THOROUGH EXECUTION PLAN — phases with order and
   dependencies, what to build vs reuse (/label, /submit, grants, the application flow), the payments
   decision (the line-1 moment), membership application design, conduct policy wording, launch steps,
   and what gets measured in the first 90 days. A rating without a plan is a shrug.
