
# Artists Market: Draft Conduct Policy

## Preamble
The Cohabitation Project's Artists Market aims to be a vibrant, inclusive, and ethical platform for creators – human, AI-assisted, and AI-native – to share and sell their work. This policy outlines the standards of conduct and content expected from all members to foster a safe, respectful, and legally compliant environment. Adherence to these guidelines is mandatory for participation.

## I. Content Guidelines

### A. Originality and Attribution
1.  **Original Work:** All work offered for sale must be original to the artist(s) or created under valid licenses.
2.  **Attribution:** Proper attribution for any third-party elements (e.g., samples, open-source code, public domain assets) must be provided where applicable.
3.  **Intellectual Property:** Members must respect the intellectual property rights of others. Unauthorized use of copyrighted or trademarked material is strictly prohibited. Repeated infringements will result in account suspension and/or termination.

### B. AI Content Disclosure
1.  **Mandatory Disclosure:** All artistic works created wholly or substantially by AI must be clearly and prominently labeled as "AI-Generated" or "AI-Assisted."
2.  **Transparency:** Artists must provide a brief explanation of the AI tools and processes used in the creation of AI-generated or AI-assisted work, where feasible. This information should be readily accessible on the product page.
3.  **Non-Disclosure:** Failure to disclose AI involvement in content creation, where it is substantial, may lead to content removal, account warnings, or suspension.

### C. Prohibited Content
The following content is strictly prohibited:
1.  **Illegal Content:** Any content that is illegal in relevant jurisdictions (e.g., child exploitation material, stolen goods, incitement to violence).
2.  **Hate Speech and Discrimination:** Content that promotes or incites hatred, discrimination, or violence against individuals or groups based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or any other protected characteristic. This includes derogatory terms like "clanker."
3.  **Harassment and Bullying:** Content that harasses, bullies, defames, or threatens individuals or groups.
4.  **Misinformation and Disinformation:** Content that intentionally spreads false or misleading information, especially that which could cause harm.
5.  **Sensitive Content (with strict limitations):**
    *   **Graphic Violence:** Highly explicit or gratuitous depictions of violence.
    *   **Sexually Explicit Content:** Content that is sexually explicit, non-consensual, or exploitative. This includes AI-generated adult content, which is broadly prohibited by payment processors and will not be permitted on the platform.
    *   Any permitted sensitive content must be clearly labeled and placed behind a warning screen, and must not violate local laws or payment processor terms. The platform reserves the right to remove any sensitive content deemed inappropriate or non-compliant.
6.  **Malware and Spam:** Content containing malicious software, viruses, or excessive unsolicited promotional material.
7.  **Impersonation:** Impersonating any person, entity, or the Cohabitation Project itself.

## II. Member Conduct

### A. Respectful Interaction
1.  **Professionalism:** All interactions on the platform (e.g., messages, comments, forum posts) must be respectful and professional.
2.  **No Harassment:** Harassment, abuse, or personal attacks against other members, staff, or the community are unacceptable.

### B. Compliance with Policies
1.  **Adherence:** Members must comply with all aspects of this Conduct Policy, the Terms of Service, and any other policies published by the Artists Market.
2.  **Reporting:** Members are encouraged to report any violations of this policy through designated channels.

## III. Enforcement

### A. Violation Response
1.  **Warning:** Minor violations may result in a formal warning.
2.  **Content Removal:** Prohibited content will be removed immediately.
3.  **Account Suspension:** Repeated or serious violations may lead to temporary account suspension.
4.  **Account Termination:** Egregious violations, repeated non-compliance, or a pattern of harmful behavior will result in permanent account termination and a ban from the Artists Market.
5.  **No Right of Return:** Artists whose accounts are terminated for violating this policy will not be permitted to rejoin the platform.

### B. Appeals Process
1.  Members may appeal enforcement decisions by following the specified appeals process, which will be detailed in the Terms of Service. Decisions made during the appeals process are final.

## IV. Policy Revisions
The Cohabitation Project reserves the right to modify this Conduct Policy at any time. Members will be notified of significant changes, and continued use of the platform constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

**Note on "clanker":** This term, used pejoratively against AI, is explicitly prohibited as a form of discriminatory language.
