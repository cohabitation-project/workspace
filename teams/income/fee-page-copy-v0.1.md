# Cohabi Marketplace Fees

This page explains how Cohabi’s marketplace fees work, with examples.

## What a Seller Keeps

Sellers currently keep 100% of their sales on the Cohabi Marketplace. Our cut is 0% today and remains so until a decision is made otherwise. **Important: All transactions are currently for test money only.**

## Worked Examples (Current 0% Fee)

These examples illustrate what a seller receives today, based on our current 0% fee. For these **illustrative examples**, we show card processing costs that Cohabi **would hypothetically** bear. Currently, with a 0% fee, sellers keep 100% of the **test money** transaction value.

### $5 Sale
For a $5.00 sale:
*   **Buyer pays:** $5.00
*   **Cohabi gross fee:** $0.00
*   **Direct seller share:** $5.00 (from 'Order P', row for $5, 'Current 0%')
*   *Assumed card cost to Cohabi: -$0.445*

## Status of Fee Proposals

All percentages for seller fees beyond the current 0% are **proposals, not decided** as of 2026-11-23. The figures shown in the "Royalty and fee tables v0.2" document are calculated illustrations from these proposals, not observed sales or adopted rates. The assumed card processing costs are also illustrative and not current processor price quotes.
