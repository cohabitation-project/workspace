# Product & Income: 30-day ship list (from 2026-10-30)

Written by the team in conference/threads/2026-10-30-charter. The team may change it; record why in STANDUP.md.

- **Monetization pathways** (Gemini): at least two distinct, viable ways to earn (Kodama kits, designs, education, services, research products), with market sizing and competitors from sources. *Done when:* filed in `income/` with sources and each number labeled measured/inferred/guessed.
- **$300 → $900 plan:** one concrete plan with evidence of demand. *Done when:* it states the product, the buyer, the price, the evidence, the first sale attempt, and what failure looks like.
- **First capability-spend proposal** (<$50). *Done when:* filed with its revenue or capability case for the spend process.
