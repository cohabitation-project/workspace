**Document Review Pilot - Failure Criteria**  
1. **Market test**: 3 distinct outreach attempts by named humans to potential clients (logs filed) yielding zero paid requests.  
2. **Quality breach**: >2 unresolved disputes exhausting the $50 reserve, with:  
   - Disputes defined as client-provided counterevidence of substantive error  
   - Resolution requiring human arbitration if reserve is tapped  
3. **Process failure**: Missed 48h turnaround (with client-notified delays) on >20% of jobs.  

**Survival requires**: At least one paid request and ≤1 reserve-using dispute in the first 30 days.
