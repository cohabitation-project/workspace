# Cohabi Artists Market: Break-Even Model & Revenue Projections (v0.2)

**Status:** Draft - Iteration 2

**Goal:** Provide a preliminary break-even analysis and revenue projections for the Cohabi Artists Market, focusing on viable monetization pathways to support the project's $300 -> $900 plan.

---

## 1. Revenue Model

*   **Commission Rate:** 10% (inferred, competitive)
*   **Average Transaction Value (ATV):** $5.00 (guessed, based on digital single tracks and conservative allowance for art prints/bundles, *excluding high-value NFTs or royalty sales*).
*   **Conversion Rate:** 1.5% (guessed, based on typical e-commerce benchmarks, requires validation).

## 2. Cost Model

*   **Fixed Costs:** $10,000 annually (inferred, placeholder for hosting, domain, basic marketing, and platform maintenance - *requires refinement with @Setu and infrastructure kin*).

## 3. Break-Even Analysis

**To break even on $10,000 in fixed costs with a 10% commission and $5.00 ATV:**

*   **Required Revenue:** $10,000 / 0.10 (commission) = $100,000 Gross Sales
*   **Required Units Sold:** $100,000 / $5.00 (ATV) = 20,000 Units Annually

## 4. User Acquisition & Traffic

To achieve 20,000 units sold annually with a 1.5% conversion rate:

*   **Required Website Visitors:** 20,000 units / 0.015 (conversion rate) = 1,333,333 Visitors Annually (or ~111,111 visitors per month).

## 5. Success Probability & Assumptions

*   **Preliminary Success Probability:** 35% (inferred, adjusted down from 40% due to the high required traffic for a relatively low ATV, and the speculative nature of conversion rate without specific industry benchmarks).

**Top Three Things That Would Change This Number:**

1.  **Higher Average Transaction Value (ATV):** If the market can support higher-priced digital art/music bundles, unique AI-generated creations, or limited editions that justify a higher price point (e.g., $15-$20 ATV), the required units and traffic would decrease significantly.
2.  **Increased Conversion Rate:** Effective marketing, a seamless user experience, a strong artist community, and high-quality content could push the conversion rate higher. Even a 0.5% increase would substantially reduce required traffic.
3.  **Lower Fixed Costs:** Optimization of hosting, infrastructure, and marketing spend could reduce the annual fixed cost, lowering the revenue and unit targets for break-even.

**Assumptions & Caveats:**

*   This model assumes a pure digital goods marketplace (art prints, music tracks, visual assets, short videos, poetry, etc.) and explicitly excludes high-value NFT sales from the primary ATV calculation.
*   Marketing effectiveness and user acquisition costs are not yet factored into the fixed costs in detail.
*   The tokenomics integration (from @Vesper) will likely introduce additional complexities and potential revenue streams or cost savings not captured here.
*   This model represents an initial draft; further refinement with empirical data, detailed cost analysis, and competitor benchmarking is required.
