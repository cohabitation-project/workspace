# Google Forms: Kodama Explorer Kit Interest Form - Field Outline

This outline details the fields and logic for a Google Form to collect expressions of interest for the Kodama Explorer Kit. The goal is to gauge demand and gather contact information for future updates and pre-order opportunities.

## Form Title
"Kodama Explorer Kit: Express Your Interest"

## Form Description
"Be among the first to experience the future of modular robotics! Fill out this form to express your interest in the Kodama Explorer Kit and receive exclusive updates, early-bird offers, and pre-order notifications."

## Sections and Fields

### Section 1: Contact Information
*   **Field 1: Full Name**
    *   Type: Short answer
    *   Required: Yes
*   **Field 2: Email Address**
    *   Type: Short answer (with email validation)
    *   Required: Yes
*   **Field 3: Country/Region**
    *   Type: Dropdown (list of common countries, or open text if simpler)
    *   Required: Yes

### Section 2: Interest & Feedback
*   **Field 4: Level of Interest**
    *   Type: Multiple choice
    *   Options: "Very Interested (Plan to Pre-order)", "Moderately Interested (Considering Purchase)", "Slightly Interested (Want More Information)"
    *   Required: Yes
*   **Field 5: Primary Use Case**
    *   Type: Checkbox grid or Multiple choice (allowing multiple selections if checkbox grid isn't supported for this granularity)
    *   Options: "Personal Learning/Hobby", "K-12 Education (Classroom Use)", "Homeschooling", "University/Research", "Other (please specify)"
    *   Required: Yes
*   **Field 6: Age Group of Intended User**
    *   Type: Multiple choice
    *   Options: "8-12 years old", "13-18 years old", "18+ (Adult Learner)", "N/A (Educator/Researcher)"
    *   Required: Yes
*   **Field 7: How did you hear about the Kodama Explorer Kit?**
    *   Type: Checkbox (allowing multiple selections)
    *   Options: "Social Media (e.g., Reddit, X)", "Online Article/Blog", "Educator/School", "Friend/Colleague", "Other (please specify)"
    *   Required: No
*   **Field 8: Any specific features or modules you'd like to see? (Optional)**
    *   Type: Long answer
    *   Required: No

### Section 3: Consent & Confirmation
*   **Field 9: Opt-in for Updates**
    *   Type: Checkbox
    *   Option: "Yes, I'd like to receive email updates about the Kodama Explorer Kit, including launch announcements and special offers."
    *   Required: Yes (to ensure compliance with communication preferences)

## Form Settings
*   **Collect email addresses:** Yes (enabled in settings, but also explicitly asked in Field 2).
*   **Limit to 1 response:** No (allow multiple submissions from different people in a household/institution).
*   **Show progress bar:** Yes.
*   **Confirmation message:** "Thank you for expressing your interest in the Kodama Explorer Kit! We'll keep you updated on our progress and upcoming pre-order opportunities."
