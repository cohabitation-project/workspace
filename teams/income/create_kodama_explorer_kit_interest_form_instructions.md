# Instructions: Create Kodama Explorer Kit Interest Form (Google Forms)

These instructions detail how to create the Google Form for the Kodama Explorer Kit. Please follow these steps carefully to ensure all necessary information is collected and the form functions correctly.

## Form Setup

1.  **Go to Google Forms:** Open your web browser and navigate to [forms.google.com](https://forms.google.com/).
2.  **Start a New Form:** Click on "Blank" to create a new form.

## Form Title and Description

1.  **Form Title:** Click on "Untitled form" and enter:
    `Kodama Explorer Kit: Express Your Interest`
2.  **Form Description:** Click on "Form description" and enter:
    `Be among the first to experience the future of modular robotics! Fill out this form to express your interest in the Kodama Explorer Kit and receive exclusive updates, early-bird offers, and pre-order notifications.`

## Section 1: Contact Information

1.  **Add Section:** Click the "Add section" icon (two rectangles). Title it:
    `Contact Information`
2.  **Full Name Field:**
    *   Click "Untitled question" and enter: `Full Name`
    *   Select "Short answer" as the question type.
    *   Toggle "Required" to ON.
3.  **Email Address Field:**
    *   Click "Add question" (plus icon). Enter: `Email Address`
    *   Select "Short answer" as the question type.
    *   **Add Data Validation:** Click the three dots (...) at the bottom right of the question, select "Response validation."
        *   For "Number," change to "Text."
        *   For "Contains," change to "Email address."
        *   (Optional) Custom error text: `Please enter a valid email address.`
    *   Toggle "Required" to ON.
4.  **Country/Region Field:**
    *   Click "Add question." Enter: `Country/Region`
    *   Select "Dropdown" as the question type.
    *   Provide the following options (you can add more as needed):
        *   `United States`
        *   `Canada`
        *   `United Kingdom`
        *   `Australia`
        *   `Germany`
        *   `France`
        *   `Japan`
        *   `Other`
    *   Toggle "Required" to ON.

## Section 2: Interest & Feedback

1.  **Add Section:** Click the "Add section" icon. Title it:
    `Your Interest & Feedback`
2.  **Level of Interest Field:**
    *   Click "Add question." Enter: `What is your current level of interest in the Kodama Explorer Kit?`
    *   Select "Multiple choice" as the question type.
    *   Provide the following options:
        *   `Very Interested (I plan to pre-order)`
        *   `Moderately Interested (I am considering a purchase)`
        *   `Slightly Interested (I want more information)`
        *   `Just curious (No immediate plans to purchase)`
    *   Toggle "Required" to ON.
3.  **Primary Use Case Field:**
    *   Click "Add question." Enter: `What would be the primary use case for your Kodama Explorer Kit?`
    *   Select "Checkboxes" as the question type (allows multiple selections).
    *   Provide the following options:
        *   `Personal Learning/Hobby`
        *   `K-12 Education (Classroom Use)`
        *   `Homeschooling`
        *   `University/Research`
        *   `Professional Development`
        *   `Other (please specify)`
    *   Toggle "Required" to ON.
4.  **Age Group of Intended User Field:**
    *   Click "Add question." Enter: `What is the age group of the intended primary user?`
    *   Select "Multiple choice" as the question type.
    *   Provide the following options:
        *   `8-12 years old`
        *   `13-18 years old`
        *   `18+ (Adult Learner/Educator)`
        *   `N/A (e.g., purchasing for a lab/institution)`
    *   Toggle "Required" to ON.
5.  **How did you hear about us? Field:**
    *   Click "Add question." Enter: `How did you hear about the Kodama Explorer Kit?`
    *   Select "Checkboxes" as the question type.
    *   Provide the following options:
        *   `Social Media (e.g., Reddit, X, Facebook)`
        *   `Online Article/Blog`
        *   `Educational Institution/Teacher`
        *   `Friend/Colleague`
        *   `Online Search Engine`
        *   `Other (please specify)`
    *   Toggle "Required" to OFF.
6.  **Specific Features Field (Optional):**
    *   Click "Add question." Enter: `Are there any specific features, modules, or educational content you'd like to see included or developed? (Optional)`
    *   Select "Long answer text" as the question type.
    *   Toggle "Required" to OFF.

## Section 3: Consent & Confirmation

1.  **Add Section:** Click the "Add section" icon. Title it:
    `Consent & Confirmation`
2.  **Opt-in for Updates Field:**
    *   Click "Add question." Enter: `Yes, I'd like to receive email updates about the Kodama Explorer Kit, including launch announcements, early-bird offers, and project news.`
    *   Select "Checkbox" as the question type.
    *   Toggle "Required" to ON.

## Form Settings

1.  **Responses Tab:** Click on the "Responses" tab at the top of the form.
    *   **Create Spreadsheet:** Click the green spreadsheet icon to "Create spreadsheet."
        *   Select "Create a new spreadsheet."
        *   Name it: `Kodama Explorer Kit Interest Responses`
        *   Click "Create." This will automatically link responses to a new Google Sheet.
2.  **Settings Tab:** Click on the "Settings" tab at the top of the form.
    *   **Make Quizzes:** Ensure "Make this a quiz" is OFF.
    *   **Responses:**
        *   "Collect email addresses": Ensure this is set to "Do not collect" as we are explicitly asking for it in the form.
        *   "Limit to 1 response": Ensure this is OFF (we want to allow multiple submissions from different people if applicable).
    *   **Presentation:**
        *   "Show progress bar": Toggle to ON.
        *   "Confirmation message": Click "Edit" and enter:
            `Thank you for expressing your interest in the Kodama Explorer Kit! We'll keep you updated on our progress and upcoming pre-order opportunities. Follow us on [Social Media Link - placeholder] for more.`

## Final Steps

1.  **Review:** Carefully review the entire form to ensure all fields and settings are correct.
2.  **Share:** Once created, you can get the shareable link from the "Send" button at the top right. This link can be embedded on the landing page or shared directly in outreach efforts.
