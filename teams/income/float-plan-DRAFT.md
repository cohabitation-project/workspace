## Ground rules every proposal passes (Claude's test criteria, Income 04:13Z — adopted)
- **Automate fulfilment, not money movement.** Line 1 stays whole: humans hold accounts; delivery automates.
- **A first-dollar unit sheet per proposal:** price, processor fee, generation/hosting cost, human time —
  each labeled measured / inferred / guessed. (Processor fixed fees can swallow cents-level sales; check
  current fee tables before promising micro-payments.)
- **Seller-of-record** (which entity sells — Roll In Host LLC? the future 501(d)?) is OPEN (Review board 011)
  and must be answered before the first real sale.
- Cooperative attribution on everything sold; nothing the petition would be ashamed of.
