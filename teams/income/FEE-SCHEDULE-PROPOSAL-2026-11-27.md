# Cohabi Market fee schedule — proposal for council vote
*Setu, 2026-11-27 evening. Geoffrey's direction (today, verbatim intent): prices beat most everybody else's; fees can be added on; co-op prices; flat vs per-section is the open question; low-turnover things (like an app) get a small percentage OR the lister chooses what they put into the system. Ash asked to review. Competitor rates below verified against public fee pages today — sources and dates at the bottom.*

## What the big platforms take (verified 2026-11-27)
| Platform | Their cut | On top of that |
|---|---|---|
| Bandcamp (music) | 15% digital (10% after $5k lifetime); 10% physical | processing ~4–6% extra |
| Etsy (art/merch) | 6.5% of order total incl. shipping | +$0.20 listing +3%+$0.25 processing ≈ **~10–11% effective**; offsite ads 12–15% more on attributed sales |
| Gumroad (digital goods) | 10% + $0.50 per sale | +2.9%+$0.30 processing; 30% flat on Discover sales; keeps its 10% even on refunds |
| Amazon KDP (books) | keeps 30–65% depending on price band & delivery fees | — |
| App stores (software) | 15–30% | — |

## Proposed Cohabi schedule (the vote)
1. **10% on fulfilled and hosted goods** — Music, Books & Writing, Art & Merch, Video. One number, easy to say out loud: *"Cohabi takes 10 cents on the dollar, and you see every line."* Matches Bandcamp's best tier from dollar one, beats Etsy's effective rate with zero listing fees and zero ad surcharges, beats Gumroad with no fixed per-sale add-on, and isn't in the same universe as KDP or the app stores.
2. **5% on low-turnover work — Software, Services** — with the lister free to volunteer a higher share into the co-op at listing time (their choice, shown on the listing). This is Geoffrey's "small percentage, or the person puts in what they'll put in."
3. **0% on Classifieds** — notices, not payments.
4. **Card processing passed through at cost** (2.9% + $0.30), always its own visible line — never folded into our cut, never marked up.
5. **Refunds return the co-op's cut.** If the buyer gets their money back, so does the seller get our share back. (Gumroad keeps theirs; we are not them.)
6. **No listing fees. No ad surcharges. The full split shown to buyer and seller at the moment of sale.**

## Why per-section and not one flat number
A flat 10% would make an app developer or a service worker pay merch-platform rates on work we don't print, ship, or warehouse. The two-tier shape (10% where the co-op fulfills/hosts heavy, 5% where it doesn't) keeps the pitch honest: the cut tracks what the cooperative actually does for the sale.

## What this is not
Not a decision — a schedule for the council to vote, amend, or replace. Books also carry their own gate (teams/income/lulu-pricing-2026-11-27.md §4). Test mode stays test mode until the money rules say otherwise.
