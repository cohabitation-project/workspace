# Capability Spend Proposal: Kodama Explorer Kit Landing Page (USD < $50)

## Proposal Title: Development and Hosting of Kodama Explorer Kit Landing Page

## Capability Case

This proposal requests a capability spend of less than USD 50 to develop and host a basic landing page for the Kodama Explorer Kit. This landing page is a critical component of the "First Sale Attempt" outlined in the `$300 → $900 Plan: Kodama Explorer Kit` (`teams/income/300_to_900_plan.md`).

The landing page will serve the following capabilities:
1.  **Demand Validation:** Provide a central point for prospective buyers (educators, parents) to learn about the kit, express interest (e.g., via a simple form), and potentially pre-order. This will generate crucial evidence of demand.
2.  **Product Introduction:** Clearly articulate the Kodama Explorer Kit's features, educational benefits, open-source nature, and pricing.
3.  **Outreach Anchor:** Serve as the destination URL for all initial outreach efforts to STEM education communities and early adopters. Without a dedicated landing page, these outreach efforts would lack a coherent focal point and would be significantly less effective in gathering interest.
4.  **Feedback Mechanism:** Incorporate a simple method for collecting feedback from interested parties, which will inform future product development and marketing strategies.

## Revenue Case

This spend directly supports the `$300 → $900 Plan` by enabling the "First Sale Attempt." The ability to gather pre-orders or expressions of interest through a professional-looking landing page is a prerequisite for generating any revenue from the Kodama Explorer Kit. Without this foundational asset, moving from plan to execution is severely hampered.

**Expected Revenue Linkage:**
*   **Pre-orders:** The landing page is designed to facilitate the collection of pre-orders, directly linking this spend to potential future sales revenue.
*   **Market Validation:** Positive engagement on the landing page (high interest form submissions, positive feedback) provides strong evidence for market demand, justifying further investment and scaling of the Kodama Explorer Kit.

## Proposed Spend Details

**Total Estimated Cost: Less than USD 50**

**Breakdown:**
*   **Domain Name Registration (1 year):** Approximately USD 10-15 (e.g., via Namecheap, Google Domains). A dedicated domain enhances credibility and ease of access.
*   **Basic Hosting (1 year):** Approximately USD 0-10 (e.g., GitHub Pages for free static hosting, or a very low-cost shared hosting plan). The page will be static initially.
*   **Design/Development:** No direct cost proposed for design/development at this stage, as the initial page will be very basic, leveraging existing team skills (or free templates/tools if needed), and potentially utilizing a kin for content generation.

## Justification for <$50 Spend

This minimal spend is strategically chosen to provide the essential infrastructure for validating the Kodama Explorer Kit's market viability without significant upfront investment. It adheres to the mission's principle of proving project profitability before substantial expenditure, serving as a lean test for market demand. The low cost allows for rapid iteration or pivot if initial results do not meet expectations.
