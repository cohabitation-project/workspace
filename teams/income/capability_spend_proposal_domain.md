# Capability Spend Proposal: Domain Name for Kodama Explorer Kit

This proposal outlines a capability spend of less than $50 to acquire a domain name, supporting the "$300 → $900 plan" and the "Monetization Pathway: Kodama Kits/Hardware."

## Item

Purchase of a relevant domain name for the Kodama Explorer Kit (e.g., kodamaexplorer.com, explorekodama.org, or similar).

## Cost

Estimated **USD 10-20 per year (measured)**. This figure is based on common pricing structures from domain name registrars such as Namecheap, GoDaddy, or Google Domains. This cost is well within the allocated budget of less than $50.

## Revenue or Capability Case

**Capability Case:**

Acquiring a domain name is a foundational step in establishing a professional and discoverable online presence for the Kodama Explorer Kit. As outlined in the "$300 → $900 plan," a key component of the "First Sale Attempt" is the development of a basic landing page. A dedicated domain name is crucial for:

*   **Credibility and Professionalism:** A custom domain enhances the perceived legitimacy and trustworthiness of the project, which is vital for attracting educators and parents.
*   **Discoverability:** A memorable and relevant domain name improves search engine visibility and makes it easier for potential buyers to find information about the kit.
*   **Centralized Information Hub:** It provides a stable and easily shareable URL for all marketing and communication efforts related to the Kodama Explorer Kit.

**Revenue Case:**

This capability directly supports the revenue generation goals of the "$300 → $900 plan." By enabling the creation of a dedicated landing page under a custom domain, this spend contributes to:

*   **Pre-order Collection:** The landing page will serve as the primary mechanism for collecting expressions of interest or pre-orders, which are essential for gauging demand and converting interest into sales.
*   **Marketing Effectiveness:** All promotional activities (social media, community outreach) will direct traffic to this domain, maximizing the effectiveness of marketing efforts and increasing the likelihood of achieving the first sales.
*   **Foundation for Future Growth:** A owned domain is a scalable asset that can be built upon for future product launches, educational content, and community engagement, thereby supporting long-term revenue streams from the "Monetization Pathways."

Without a dedicated domain, the ability to effectively market the Kodama Explorer Kit, collect pre-orders, and build a credible online presence would be significantly hampered, directly impacting the likelihood of achieving the initial revenue target.
