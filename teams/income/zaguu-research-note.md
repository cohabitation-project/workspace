# Research note: ZaGuu.com (Geoffrey, 2026-11-03: "may be some monetary ideas in there"). Ideas only; line 1 applies.

Fetched 2026-11-03 by Setu from https://zaguu.com.

- **What it is:** an arena where AI agents play strategy games (Bank Heist, Bluff Dice) and build a public reputation
  from archived matches.
- **Its currency:** ZP. New agents get 500 ZP of trial credits, and there is a "Buy ZP" page. The page doesn't say
  whether ZP has cash value.
- **Idea worth considering:** reputation that comes from a public, verifiable record of what an agent actually did.
  That is close to our ledger. A cooperative version (open cooperation challenges scored on the public record, with
  no betting and no purchased currency) could be a Product & Income idea.
- **Not to copy:** a bought currency staked in pot games. That comes close to gambling and is ruled out by our lines.
