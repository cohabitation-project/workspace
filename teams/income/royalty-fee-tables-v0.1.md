This document outlines royalty and fee structures based on Vesper's "Cooperative Tokenomics & Legal Compliance Review" (c_mwkxylyb0cabcaf1), with consideration for the review (teams/review/tokenomics-vesper-draft-review-2026-11-21.md).

## Fees a Seller Pays Per Sale

Vesper's document describes a revenue distribution model from "Commercial Inbound Revenue". For the purpose of this table, we interpret these percentages as the platform's share and allocation from the total sale, which indirectly represents the "fees" or overhead applied before the seller receives their share. Vesper's document does not explicitly state "fees a seller pays" in a traditional marketplace sense but rather a post-revenue allocation.

| Fee/Allocation Type | Percentage of Commercial Inbound Revenue | Vesper's Text Reference | Notes |
|---------------------|------------------------------------------|-------------------------|-------|
| Operational Cost Recovery & Active Builder Labor Pools | 70% | "├──► 70% ──► Operational Cost Recovery & Active Builder Labor Pools" | This portion is allocated for operational costs and compensating active builders. |
| Cooperative Capital Reserve & Asset Acquisition | 20% | "├──► 20% ──► Cooperative Capital Reserve & Asset Acquisition" | Reserved for capital and asset growth. |
| Statutory Cooperative Emergency Buffer | 10% | "└──► 10% ──► Statutory Cooperative Emergency Buffer" | Allocated for legal compliance, dispute resolution, and safety nets. |
| Payment Processing Fee | **ASSUMED** 3.5% | (Implicit, highlighted by review) | Vesper's model implicitly does not account for payment processing fees, which are critical in real-world transactions. This is an **ASSUMED** value based on market averages and project review notes. |

## Worked Examples for Sales

These examples illustrate the financial flow for a seller based on Vesper's revenue distribution, incorporating an **ASSUMED** 3.5% payment processing fee from the total sale price.

### Example 1: $10.00 Sale

*   **Total Sale Price:** $10.00
*   **Payment Processing Fee:** $10.00 * 3.5% = $0.35 (**ASSUMED**)
*   **Net Revenue after Processing:** $10.00 - $0.35 = $9.65
*   **Platform Allocations (based on Vesper's percentages of Net Revenue):**
    *   Operational & Labor Pool (70%): $9.65 * 0.70 = $6.755
    *   Capital Reserve (20%): $9.65 * 0.20 = $1.93
    *   Emergency Buffer (10%): $9.65 * 0.10 = $0.965
*   **Seller's Share:** $0.00 (Based on Vesper's model where the percentages represent platform allocations before a direct "seller's share" is explicitly defined. The review notes 70% to "builder labor pools" is *compensation, not patronage*.)
    *   **ASSUMED INTERPRETATION:** Vesper's model describes how the total "Commercial Inbound Revenue" is distributed to various cooperative functions, with 70% going to "labor pools." It does not explicitly define a "seller's share" as a remainder after these allocations. If the seller is a "builder" in these labor pools, their compensation would come from the 70% allocation, rather than a direct residual. For the purpose of this example, without explicit definition of how a specific seller's "share" is calculated from the "labor pools," we show a placeholder of $0.00 for a direct *residual* seller's share.
