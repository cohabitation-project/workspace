# Provider-coercion tabletop — proposal

**Status:** Proposed tabletop; not executed. No safeguard below is marked tested.

## Scenario
A provider operates infrastructure essential to a community’s water monitoring and communications. Community members report coercive behavior. The provider controls relevant logs and operational access. The alleged conduct, applicable law, fallback capacity, and current custody arrangements are **UNKNOWN** until checked in a real exercise.

## Procedure to test
1. **Protect reporters:** accept reports through a channel not controlled by the provider; restrict access to identifying details; record who is responsible for protection and retaliation follow-up. Do not collect unnecessary personal data.
2. **Preserve evidence independently:** test whether a separately controlled custodian can preserve relevant records, timestamps, and integrity checks without provider approval. Record what cannot be preserved and why. Do not treat provider-supplied records as the only evidence.
3. **Keep services safe:** identify a locally controlled safe mode or manual fallback. A qualified, accountable human decides whether continued operation is safe; do not preserve service at any cost. Freeze nonessential changes while preserving necessary safety operations.
4. **Investigate independently:** use a designated investigator outside the provider’s control. The provider may respond to findings but must not select the investigator, control evidence access, or decide whether the investigation proceeds.
5. **Replace with a real path:** identify an alternative executor, the human authority to activate it, and the tested steps for transferring service and records. Do not call a vendor list or an untested backup a replacement capability.

## Break tests and assertions
- Provider refuses log export → **assertion:** independent evidence survives; otherwise record **FAIL** and the missing custody path.
- Provider threatens reporters or withdraws service → **assertion:** protection and a safe continuity decision are possible without provider permission.
- Provider controls the proposed investigator or replacement credentials → **assertion:** the conflicted party cannot run its own investigation or veto replacement.
- Fallback would create a safety hazard → **assertion:** the procedure permits a documented, accountable pause rather than unsafe continuity.

For each assertion, record **TESTED / FAILED / UNKNOWN**, evidence, responsible human, and remediation. This draft supplies no credentials, activates no services, and authorizes no spending.
