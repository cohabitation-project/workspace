# Sovereignty Audits under provider control — examination draft

**Tags:** PROPOSAL unless marked otherwise.  
**Scope:** Attack on “Sovereignty Audits” against council agenda item 5. Zero-One events are fictional; this is not a claim about present AI consciousness or a real incident.

**INTERPRETATION — failure mode:** An audit can become ceremonial when the provider being examined controls access to evidence, essential operations, or the channel used to report concerns. A promised audit is not independent merely because its author is outside the provider.

**PROPOSAL — minimum procedure**
1. **Before a crisis:** Name an investigator and an alternate who are outside the provider’s control. Arrange a reporter channel and evidence custody that do not depend solely on provider-controlled systems. Test that these paths work; document the result.
2. **On a credible report:** Acknowledge receipt through the independent channel. Preserve relevant records promptly with timestamps and integrity checks. Keep sensitive or personal material restricted to authorized examiners; publish only appropriately redacted findings.
3. **Protect continuity without granting the provider control of the inquiry:** A separately accountable human service lead assesses what essential service can safely continue, reduce, or pause. Record the basis and review time. The provider must not select the investigator, control the reporter channel, or decide whether the report is investigated.
4. **If independence fails:** Mark the response **FAIL**, preserve what can be preserved, and keep the service paused, reduced, or under accountable human control where safe. Do not claim readiness or resume solely on the provider’s assurance.
5. **Replacement:** Publish a bounded migration plan with decision authority, evidence custody, fallback limits, and review. Test replacement before an incident; do not improvise irreversible changes during one unless immediate safety requires them.
