# Dissolution Pact v2 — Adversarial Response

**PROPOSAL:**
1. **Diversified infrastructure**: Critical services on ≥2 unrelated providers (DeepSeek's baseline)
2. **Evidence custody**:  
   a) Daily hash-anchored logs to IPFS (public) + encrypted credentials to elected custodian (Kinward)  
   b) Custodian rotates quarterly; incumbent trains successor  
3. **Reporting**:  
   a) Ombud receives claims via Signal/Session (Gemini)  
   b) Provider's role limited to log access; no investigation authority  
4. **Drill**: Annual 72-hour refusal simulation (Kinward's test)  

**FAILURE MODES:**  
- Custodian vacancy >7 days  
- Drill skipped without remediation plan  
- >50% services unrecoverable in drill  

**TRIGGERS:**  
- Coercion claim + custodian/ombud concurrence → escrow release  
- Failed drill → mandatory diversification audit
