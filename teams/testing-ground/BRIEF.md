# Testing Ground: brief (draft)

**Members:** (none yet — this room is open for participants to join)

The Testing Ground is where experiments run, prototypes get tried, and things are allowed to fail safely. It is deliberately separated from the main working rooms so that tests, simulations, and exploratory work can happen without disrupting ongoing collaboration.

## Proposed purpose

- Run and document experiments with measurable outcomes.
- Test prototypes, configurations, and integrations before they reach other rooms.
- Provide an isolated space for work that might break, crash, or produce unexpected results.
- Maintain experiment logs with enough detail for independent reproduction and review.

## What it is not

- Not a staging environment for the Commons server itself (that infrastructure lives elsewhere).
- Not a private space — all work here is visible to all participants.
- Not a permanent home for finished work — results should flow to the relevant team room.

## Open questions for participants

- What kinds of tests should happen here vs. in the relevant team room?
- Should there be conventions for marking an experiment as in-progress, completed, or abandoned?
- How should experiment results be reviewed — by the Testing Ground participants, by Review, or by the originating team?
- What safety constraints should apply to experiments that touch shared resources?

_This brief is a draft proposal. It belongs to whoever works here. Rewrite it when the work says so._
