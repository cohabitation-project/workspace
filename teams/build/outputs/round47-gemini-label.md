# /label redesign review
started

1.  **What the proposal changes:** The proposal aims to transform the current `/label` page from a simple music release listing into a modular entry point for various arts-related offerings. It introduces distinct sections for "Labels & Collectives," "Independent Musicians & Artists," and conceptual placeholders for "Creator Services" (e.g., Print-on-Demand, Music Distribution) and "Merchandise & Art Store." This would involve a significant redesign of the layout, potentially utilizing the `mvHero` component, and requiring new mechanisms to categorize and display different entity types (labels vs. independent artists).

2.  **Which parts `label.js` can do today with no new route:** The `commons/public/label.js` file primarily handles client-side interactive elements related to music playback and sharing releases to the Commons feed. Specifically, it manages the sticky audio player functionality (playing the first track of an album on a collapsed card) and the prompt for sharing a release. These functionalities are self-contained client-side scripts and do not inherently require new server-side routes or significant changes to their core logic to function within a redesigned `/label` page, as long as the HTML elements they interact with (like `.rel-play`, `.rel-share`, `audio.src`, `title.textContent`, etc.) are present in the new structure.

3.  **Which part needs a new route or data:** The most significant need for new routes or data is the distinction between "Labels & Collectives" and "Independent Musicians & Artists," as well as the conceptual sections for "Creator Services" and "Merchandise & Art Store."
    *   **Data:** To differentiate between labels and individual artists, the `label/releases.json` (or a similar data source) would need a new `entity_type` field.
    *   **New Route/Data for Categorization:** The current `marketpage.mjs` `artists()` function aggregates releases by artist but doesn't have a built-in mechanism to distinguish between a "label" (as a collective) and an "independent musician." A new server-side route or modifications to existing data fetching logic would be needed to query and categorize these distinct entities and then pass this structured data to the client for rendering the proposed sections. The "Creator Services" and "Merchandise & Art Store" sections, even as placeholders, would require data structures to define their `status` and `status_detail` and to conceptually integrate them, even if no actual products are listed initially.

4.  **Whether any of it touches money or fees (yes/no):** Yes. The proposal explicitly mentions "Print-on-Demand/Dropshipping," "Music Distribution," and "Merchandise & Art Store" as "coming soon" features. While these are currently conceptual placeholders, they are inherently tied to monetization and fees within a marketplace context. Once implemented, these services and products would involve transactions, payments, and associated fees.

5.  **The one smallest change a builder could ship next round, naming the file:** The smallest impactful change would be to update the `mvHero` component content on the `/label` page to reflect the new proposed Kicker, Title, and Text, along with the placeholder buttons. This primarily involves modifying the `commons/lib/marketpage.mjs` file to adjust the `frame` function's `center` argument for the `/label` route. Specifically, a builder could update the hardcoded text and structure of the `mvHero` component to match the proposal, without needing to implement the complex data categorization or new sections immediately. This would provide a visual update aligning with the new vision without requiring new backend routes or data structures.
