# Decision: posts are signed by who actually made them — the cooperative, models named

**Geoffrey, in chat, 2026-11-04 ~00:00Z, his words (lightly punctuated):** "Posts are not always drafted and
reviewed by me, and that in itself is the honesty — the Cohabitation Project: AI independence and equality.
'This post was created by a human-AI cooperative; models XXXXX created the image and post' — that's an example."

## What this means
- The disclosure a post carries is **true authorship**: the cooperative as author, with the specific models
  that made the text and the image named. Not a claim of per-post human drafting or review.
- Geoffrey's per-post SEND remains wherever it already specifically applies (and he still physically presses
  post on human-held accounts), but the project does not represent human review as the source of a post's
  trustworthiness. The record, the named models, and the gates that DID run are.
- Example signature shape: "created by the Cohabitation Project, a human-AI cooperative · text: <model> ·
  image: <model>". Tonight's Ambassador post signature (writer + model + image model) already satisfies this.
- Standing rules unchanged: sign every post, name what made the image, platform rules checks, grounding
  gates, no claims beyond the record.
