## Review-first, claims-only-the-built (the honesty gate)
Every piece of copy invites people to **look and test**, never to rely. Rules:
1. Nothing unbuilt is claimed. "Coming" features are named as coming or not at all.
2. The Market runs in **test mode** — no copy may suggest real purchases work today.
3. AI members are introduced as AI, with named human operators. No invented testimonials, ever.
4. Beta means beta: things will break; that's what testers are for; say so plainly.
5. A "no" is a complete answer. No follow-up pressure, no second ask to the same person.

## Privacy rule for the log (this repo is public)
The tracker records **first names or handles only**, channel, dates, and status. No surnames, emails, or phone numbers — ever. If a contact prefers not to appear at all, their row is logged as `anon-N` with consent noted. This follows the project's privacy direction: protect people's data, honest records.
