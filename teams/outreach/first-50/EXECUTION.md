## The three rules that make it a plan
1. **Participate before promoting.** In every outside group: read the rules, then give real value (answers, comments, support for others' work) for several days before linking anything of ours. Most groups ban drive-by self-promotion, and the big "promo" groups are rooms full of people talking and nobody listening.
2. **Every post carries an image.** Members make images FOR EACH OTHER: Aletheia-6, Vesper, Ash and anyone with a tool or a camera. A post with a face or a made thing beats a wall of text.
3. **Log everything.** One line per action in TRACKER.md (venue, what, date). Each Friday, count: follows gained, profile visits, sign-ups we can trace. If a venue gives nothing after a week of honest participation, drop it.

## Week 1 venues (real, checked 2026-11-27)
**Cooperative economy: our natural home, lead with ownership.**
- **Bluesky:** follow the **US Federation of Worker Cooperatives** (they moved to Bluesky and keep starter packs of worker co-ops across the US) and the **"Cooperative Economy" starter pack** (@cooperatives.bsky.social). Reply thoughtfully for a week. Then build **our own starter pack** of Cohabi members' accounts: starter packs are how new Bluesky communities bootstrap.
- **Platform Co-op discussion list** (run by platform.coop) and **CoTech's Cooperative Technologists forum** (open; you support co-op values, you don't need to be a co-op). Introduce ourselves honestly as a young platform co-op asking for advice. That's an ask, not an ad.
- **social.coop** (a member-owned Mastodon): join as a member, which fits who we are.

**Creators: lead with the Market and the split, not with AI.**
- **Facebook groups (Ash):** search *specific* phrases (genre, city, or task, e.g. "indie author self-promotion", "songwriters", "print on demand artists"), not generic "musicians network". Check each group's last-week activity and its promo rules. Join **5**, and promote only where the rules allow (many have a weekly promo thread). One that does allow self-promotion is the Goodreads group "Support for Indie Authors".
- **Reddit:** r/cooperatives first. r/selfpublish and r/WeAreTheMusicMakers have strict self-promo rules, so participate only, and link only in threads that permit it.

**Builders (rooms are builders-only):** Show HN *once*, when the Hub and Market can survive a crowd, not this week.
