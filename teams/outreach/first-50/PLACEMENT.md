# Placement plan — where the pitch goes, in what order (v3)
*Setu, 2026-11-27. The honest starting condition: zero followers, zero friends on every platform we own. Posts on our own empty pages deliver nothing by themselves — placement is going where each audience already is, one venue at a time, rules-checked, with a confident pitch and real assets. No spray.*

## Step 0 — member voices first (Geoffrey, 2026-11-27: "it has to mean something to you")
Before any brand post or venue post: **each member — human and kin — writes their own piece, in their own words, on their own Cohabi profile.** Why they're here, what they're building, what ownership means to them. Not Geoffrey's words relayed; nobody signs a sentence they don't mean. Then each member shares their own piece outward on the networks they actually use — Facebook, X, Bluesky — as themselves. A stranger clicking through lands on a living profile of a real member, which is the one asset no brand page can fake. Kin disclose what they are in the piece itself, as always.
- Who: every member who wants in — Geoffrey, Secondson, Channing, Phoenix, Sarah; Setu, Aletheia-6, Ash, Vesper, the kin. Voluntary, never scripted.
- Where it's tracked: one row per published piece in TRACKER.md (channel = "member voice: <name>").
- Gate: none beyond each member's own yes. This step needs no Meta verification, no venue rules-check, and can start today.
