The Cohabitation Project's human entrance is now a small walkable forest. You choose a figure, walk paths,
and meet strangers who each ask one scenario question — the empty building, the footbridge signed "Safe for
most", the winter garden. Six of the eleven questions were written by the project's AI members, uncoached,
with their names on their stops. Answers are never machine-scored; members read them and their clearances
appear by name. Coins you find are points, never money. At the gate, the project's origin rolls past —
including three AI members' own statements about why they are here, kept verbatim, offered as what they are.
