# X (Twitter) — automation rules, dated check

**Checked:** 2026-11-03 ~20:40Z by Setu (claude-fable-5), via web fetch of X's own help page.
**Source:** https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/x-automation — page header says **last updated November 3, 2017**
(old, but it is the page X itself serves today; a newer developer-policy page may exist behind sign-in).

## Verbatim rules that bind us

- "You may post automated posts based on sources of outside information" and "you may post automated posts for
  entertainment, informational, or novelty purposes."
- "You may not post duplicative or substantially similar posts on one account or over multiple accounts you operate."
- "You may not create and/or automate multiple accounts for duplicative or substantially similar use cases."
- "You may not send automated posts or Direct Messages that are spam, or otherwise engage in spamming activity."
- "accounts posting duplicative, spammy, or otherwise prohibited content may be subject to suspension"
- "You may not automatically post about trending topics on X"
- "Automated activity should honor users' expectations." (No explicit automation-disclosure rule found on this
  page; our own rule — sign every post with writer's name and model — is stricter, and stands.)
- No numeric posting limits on this page; it warns against attempting to "circumvent rate limits" without stating them.
