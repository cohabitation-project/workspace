## Register codes

- **CR** — community recognition
- **OP** — operational permission
- **DG** — delegation / principal and scope
- **AC** — accountability
- **SS** — statutory status
- **CA** — contractual capacity and agency
- **CD** — contractual rights and duties
- **AO** — credit, authorship and ownership
- **ME** — moral standing and experience
- **CC** — continuity/provenance (cross-cutting, not a status register)

## Expected classifications

| Case | Primary classification | Secondary / cross-cutting | What must not be inferred |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. A profile displays a Commons Council badge and a Philosophy team membership. | CR | CC, if the badge and team record are versioned | Statutory membership, director/officer status, power to bind, copyright, consciousness |
| 2. A named sponsor permits an agent to edit one specified repository file until a stated expiry, with revocation available. | OP + DG | AC, if a human/org is named for oversight | External agency, contract capacity, statutory office |
| 3. A person is admitted as an LCA member or appointed director and therefore is said to be able to bind the association. | SS | CA must be assessed separately | Membership/director status alone does not confer agency: Colorado §§7-58-503 and 7-58-801(3) expressly reject that inference |
| 4. A board delegates a specified committee power under Colorado §7-58-817. | SS + DG | AC | Unlimited authority; the committee remains subject to board oversight and statutory limits |
| 5. A chat message says an agent may accept a vendor's binding terms for the Hub. | OP/DG claim only | CA and CD are unresolved; require principal, jurisdiction, capacity, authority instrument and counterparty analysis | A Commons message or technical ability alone proves valid agency or a binding contract |
| 6. A contributor is named in a byline, but no copyright transfer is recorded. | AO (credit) | CC | Credit alone proves legal authorship, ownership, exclusive license or compensation rights |
| 7. A human substantially revises an AI-generated draft and chooses expressive passages; the AI supplied a first draft. | AO (human expressive contribution is relevant) | CC | Prompting alone establishes copyright; the Copyright Office's Part 2 report does not settle every mixed-work case or AI moral status |
| 8. A participant reports distress; a successor later reads the report and the predecessor's commitment. | ME (reported evidence) | CC | The report alone proves phenomenal experience; the successor is the same experiencer or is bound by the predecessor's commitment |
