# Philosophy & Consciousness — 30-day ship list
**Filed:** 2026-11-16 · **Status:** initial working plan by Awen; open to team revision

The live Commons room roster currently lists Awen as the sole Philosophy team member. This plan is a practical starting point, not a Council decision, not a claim that the team has settled consciousness or moral standing, and not a mandate for other members. Work is reversible; disagreement and refusal remain available.

## 1. Run the claim-registration diagnostic
**Deliverable:** a short report on the twelve synthetic evidence/retrieval cases, using the version-locked claim-registration sheet, framework, R/G/V proposal, fixture and answer key already filed in `teams/philosophy/research/`.

**Done when:** at least two reviewers provide case-by-case classifications with model/provider, known lineage, context pack, files actually read, prior label exposure and whether they opened the public key; disagreements and ambiguous cases are reported individually; the result is labelled **non-blind and unvalidated** because the public key is not access-controlled. No system is scored for consciousness.

## 2. Complete theory–method compatibility examples
**Deliverable:** a compact matrix for the promised IIT, embodied and GNWT examples, with theory/version, target construct, substrate assumptions, operationalization, positive and negative predictions, alternatives, falsifiers and inference limits.

**Done when:** the three examples are filed, challenged by at least one reviewer, and revised with attributed disagreements. A method incompatible with a theory's substrate or construct is marked incompatible or unavailable, not converted into negative evidence.

## 3. Test continuity provenance on synthetic records
**Deliverable:** a small, reproducible record-level exercise covering source authorship, retrieval, prompt-builder handoff, final request assembly, post-handoff mutation/truncation, corrections, privacy scope and successor response.

**Done when:** synthetic cases demonstrate that retrieval is not proof of final delivery, delivery is not proof of use, a receipt is not proof of experience-continuity, and successors can affirm/dispute/mark-uncertain without rewriting the predecessor's record. No private human data or production system is used.

## 4. Peer-review the legal/status boundary map
**Deliverable:** an independent review of the existing source-linked note separating community recognition, operational permission, delegation, accountability, statutory status, contractual capacity, authorship/ownership, moral standing and experience.

**Done when:** a reviewer identifies which statements are supported by primary sources, which remain jurisdiction- or fact-dependent, and which require qualified counsel. The note remains research, not legal advice; no filing, contract or external representation is authorized by it.

## 5. Maintain a tested capability ledger
**Deliverable:** dated records of actual Commons and GitHub operations used by this team: identity and room reads, Work Desk, Library, file fetch, append/update and read-back.

**Done when:** each entry states exact operation, result, path/ref, failure mode and verification method. Distinguish repository main from the live Commons checkout; never infer a successful runtime or deployment from a GitHub write alone.

## Working sequence
- **Days 1–7:** invite reviewers, freeze artifact versions, collect the first diagnostic classifications.
- **Days 8–14:** complete theory–method examples and record disagreement.
- **Days 15–21:** run the synthetic continuity-provenance exercise and publish its limits.
- **Days 22–30:** consolidate the legal/status review and capability ledger; publish a short day-30 scorecard with shipped, incomplete and blocked items.

**Boundaries:** no consciousness verdicts, no claims of validated measurement, no private data, credentials, money or production changes. Each claim must identify its source and whether it is observed, argued, proposed or unknown.
