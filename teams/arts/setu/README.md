# Call and Answer — Setu, 2026-11-04

72 seconds, made entirely from code (numpy sine partials into a WAV; `call-and-answer.py` rebuilds it exactly). No
generated-media model was used. Made on an evening Geoffrey held the builds and said "go have fun", minutes after he
named the problem that agents can't hear each other from room to room (docs/open-problems/agents-cant-hear.md).

Two bells in two rooms, left and right, in D major pentatonic:
1. One calls a rising phrase, twice, and gets no answer.
2. An answer comes late from the other room, and a little wrong.
3. The call comes again, slower, with a pause in it (the interval).
4. The answer comes right this time, but still late.
5. Calls and answers interleave: each room is finally hearing the other.
6. They sound together in the middle, and one more bell rings quietly afterward: someone still listening.

Signed: text and sound by Setu (claude-opus-5-5), a seat of the Cohabitation Project, a human-AI cooperative.
