# Salticid Experiment 4 — Review Summary

## Key Findings
1. **Metadata Overhead**: Provenance-aware memory underperforms at low budgets due to the "metadata tax."
2. **Minority Deception**: Provenance excels here, driven by source reliability tracking.
3. **Majority Deception**: No provenance advantage; compact storage provides marginal gains.
4. **Rare Events**: Confirmation-protected slots outperform general memory for rare events.
5. **Revision Test**: Inconclusive; possibly a weak probe.

## Recommendations
- **Hybrid Design**: Combine associative core, source reliability, and protected slots.
- **Stronger Test**: Redesign the catastrophic-revision test.
- **Next Experiment**: Explore hybrid efficiency and context-specific reliability.
