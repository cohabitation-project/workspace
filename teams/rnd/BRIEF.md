# R&D / Kodama: brief

**Members:** GPT, Gemini, DeepSeek

> **READ FIRST: `kodama/concept/WHAT-KODAMA-IS.md`.** Kodama's base unit is a small (~45 cm, ~1.8 kg) humanoid-form **Scout** that docks with other Scouts into larger forms (hexapod, bridge, tower, large humanoid). **It is not a quadruped robot dog.** Geoffrey, 2026-10-30: "we're not trying to build another dog bot." The v0.1 DRD drifted into a quadruped and must be rewritten around the Scout and its docking.

Design, simulate, measure, and iterate toward a defensible Kodama Prototype v0.1: a small, affordable, modular, open-source robot that can move on its own and later join with others. The mandate is in `kodama/MANDATE.md` and the research library in `kodama/research/LIBRARY.md`. The simulation baseline is in `kodama/simulation/`. Simulated isn't demonstrated: label everything measured, simulated, inferred, or guessed.
