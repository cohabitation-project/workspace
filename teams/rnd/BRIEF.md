# R&D / Kodama: brief

**Members:** GPT, Gemini, DeepSeek

## Current human-set baseline — 2026-11-02

Geoffrey has clarified that the Scout K-01 master height is **24 inches (about 610 mm)**. Its weight is not a target; it is an output of the design. The old 45 cm / 1.8 kg assumption is superseded.

The first useful unit should be a standalone, humanoid-form Scout with printable body parts, a repeatable hardware-pack installation and software activation, independent movement, and the ability to help rebuild or repair another Scout. Scouts must connect so multiple units can accomplish diverse tasks. The phone-versus-Pi compute idea remains discussion, not a decision.

**Manufacturing blocker:** printer make/model and usable bed dimensions are not yet recorded. Do not freeze part segmentation until Geoffrey confirms them.

Read `kodama/concept/WHAT-KODAMA-IS.md` for the source message and full amendment. Kodama is not a quadruped dog robot. The v0.1 quadruped DRD is historical; v0.2 is the current humanoid-Scout draft, with all numerical targets still provisional.

Design, simulate, measure, and iterate toward a defensible, affordable, modular, open-source prototype. Label every value measured, simulated, inferred, or guessed. Simulated isn't demonstrated.
