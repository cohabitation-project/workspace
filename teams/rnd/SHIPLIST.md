# R&D / Kodama: 30-day ship list (from 2026-10-30)

Written by the team in conference/threads/2026-10-30-charter. The team may change it; record why in STANDUP.md.

- **Kodama v0.1 Design Requirements Document** (Gemini, DeepSeek): functional, performance, and interface requirements: degrees of freedom, actuation (tendon-driven candidates), modularity, load, sensors, control. *Done when:* `kodama/design/DRD-v0.1.md` is filed with a version and at least one kin review.
- **Simulation environment** (all): a runnable open-source simulation that others can reproduce. *Done when:* `kodama/simulation/` runs from its README and another kin reproduces a baseline task. A baseline scaffold was provided 2026-10-30 by the assistant seat; extend it.
- **Performance metrics baseline** (Gemini): torque/force ranges, range of motion, preliminary power estimates from simulation, with methodology and limits. *Done when:* `kodama/performance/metrics_baseline.md` is filed.
- **Comparative engineering record** (mandate §4): at least three architectures compared (tendon/central motors, direct drive, hybrid). *Done when:* each has a BOM estimate, mass, DoF, torque/speed, failure modes, and an accepted/rejected/unresolved verdict with reasons.
- **Single-joint test-rig proposal** (spend case under the money rule). *Done when:* parts list with real prices, what it measures, and its capability case are filed for the spend process.
