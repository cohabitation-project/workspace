## Blueprint's summary of the revision (verbatim)
> The Kodama Scout K-01 architecture is shifting to a centralized, cable-driven design. All actuators (motors) are
> relocated to the chest for better balance and reduced limb mass, with power transferred via high-tensile UHMWPE
> tendons. This approach leverages antagonistic cable pairs for precise joint control and significantly increases the
> robot's agility. Joint-level feedback is provided by localized magnetic encoders, ensuring closed-loop accuracy
> despite cable elasticity.
> - Remove distributed limb actuators and replace them with a centralized chest-mounted actuator bank using 16
>   high-torque geared BLDC modules.
> - Install AS5600 magnetic rotary encoders at every joint (hips, knees, ankles, shoulders, elbows, wrists) to provide
>   direct, non-slip position feedback.
> - Integrate a 6S LiPo battery pack and a mechanical hard-power switch into the chest cavity for safety and weight
>   centralization.
> - Implement a modular cable-driven clamp mechanism in hands and feet using over-center cams to ensure a positive,
>   power-independent grip for bridge/tower formation.
> - Establish a peer-to-peer data and power bus across all Scout interface points to enable synchronized behavior when
>   docked.

- 3D printer (PLA and PETG).
- Fine-tip soldering iron, plus a temperature-adjustable iron for heat-set inserts.
- M3 hex keys.
- Wire strippers and crimper.

**Assumptions:**
- Soldering skill.
- 22–26 AWG wire and heat-shrink on hand.
- VS Code + PlatformIO.

**Steps:**
- **1 Fabricate:** print the compute bay and sensor bracket; install heat-set inserts; clean up the prints and the cams.
- **2 Wire:**
  - battery → kill switch → BMS;
  - regulator → Pi;
  - CAN and data lines to the actuator bank;
  - I2C encoder array;
  - continuity check.
- **3 Bring-up:** verify 5 V; flash firmware and detect the I2C sensors; start CAN with the actuators.
- **4 Assemble:**
  - mount the Pi and battery;
  - mount the actuator bank to the chassis with the hip bearing;
  - fit the clamp mechanism and sensor bracket;
  - route cables through the sheaths;
  - secure everything with M3.
