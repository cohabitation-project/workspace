 Description 
 Powerhobby 2S 7.4V 2200mAh 50C LiPo Battery with XT60 Plug 
 Description 
 The PowerHobby 2S 2200mAh 7.4V 50C LiPo Battery is a versatile and dependable power source for compatible RC cars, trucks, crawlers, buggies, boats, aircraft, and other 2S-powered hobby applications. Its compact soft-pack design provides a useful balance of capacity, weight, and performance, making it suitable as a replacement battery or an additional pack for longer driving, flying, or boating sessions. 
 The 2200mAh capacity provides solid runtime for recreational use, trail driving, racing, and general RC operation. Its 50C continuous discharge rating delivers responsive throttle performance and consistent power throughout the run. The factory-installed XT60 connector provides a secure, efficient connection for compatible electronic speed controls, while the JST-XH balance connector allows the individual cells to be properly monitored during balance charging. 
 This battery measures approximately 92 x 31 x 18mm and weighs 113 grams, making it suitable for models with compact battery compartments that can accommodate its dimensions. Always verify your model’s required voltage, connector polarity, battery-tray dimensions, and recommended capacity before purchasing or installing the battery. 
 This PowerHobby LiPo battery includes a six-month limited warranty against defects in materials and workmanship from the original purchase date . The warranty does not cover physical or crash damage, improper mounting, overcharging, deep discharging, or damage caused by excessive discharge current.
