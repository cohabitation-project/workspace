Frequently asked questions 
Which STS3215 do I need for the SO-ARM101? 
The follower arm uses six 12 V C018 (30 kg·cm) servos. The leader arm uses 7.4 V servos with three different gear ratios so it is light to move by hand: the 12-servo set on this page contains exactly the mix from the SO-101 bill of materials. 
What is the difference between C018, C001, C044 and C046? 
C018 is the 12 V, 30 kg·cm, 1:345 version. C001 is the 7.4 V, 19 kg·cm, 1:345 version. C044 (1:191) and C046 (1:147) are 7.4 V servos with lower gear ratios: faster and easier to back-drive, less torque; the SO-101 leader arm uses them for the joints you move most. 
Is it a real Feetech servo? 
Yes. These are genuine Feetech STS3215 servos with the Feetech label, magnetic encoder and metal gearbox, the same part sold by Seeed and Waveshare. 
Can I control it from Arduino or Raspberry Pi? 
Yes. It speaks the Feetech half-duplex serial protocol. Use a bus servo board such as the BusLinker V3.0 or a Waveshare adapter for USB, or a UART plus direction pin on a microcontroller with the Feetech SCServo library. 
Does it do continuous rotation? 
Yes. Besides 0–360° position mode it supports wheel (constant speed) mode, open-loop PWM mode, step mode and multi-turn absolute position, selectable by register. 
What power supply do I need? 
12 V 5 A for six C018 servos in an arm; 7.4 V (2S LiPo or a 7.4 V supply) for the C001 / C044 / C046 models. Each servo draws about 0.9 A rated and up to 2–3 A at stall. 
Complete the build 
 One SO-ARM101 arm needs 6 STS3215 servos; a leader+follower pair needs 12. Recommended path: single servo → Waveshare adapter → cables → dual-voltage power → 6/12 kit / starter . Also see metal horns .
