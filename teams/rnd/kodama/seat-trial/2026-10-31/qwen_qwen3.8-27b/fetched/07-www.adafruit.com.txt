 Description 
If you want to make a kit you'll need some solder. This 0.1lb / 50g spool is a good amount, not too much (like 1 lb spools) and not too little (like those little 'pocket clip' vials). 
This spool of solder is the less expensive version of our popular SAC305 RoHS solder . This is lead-free solder, so great for lead-free shops, however, it's not SAC305, a formulation which some people prefer. For flux, it contains a 'no clean' rosin core. It's good for all through-hole soldering and great for any kind of SMT soldering. 
Contains Sn 99.3%, Cu 0.7% with a flux of 2.2%. 
Perfect for when you need a lead-free alternative to classic 60/40!
