 Product Description 
Upgrade your RC setup with Hitec&rsquo;s HS-5645MG Digital Servo, engineered for hobbyists who demand power, precision, and proven reliability. Featuring high-torque output, digital accuracy, and a robust metal gear train, the HS-5645MG is a top choice for demanding applications like RC vehicles, airplanes, crawlers, boats, and robotics. This servo&rsquo;s advanced digital circuitry delivers enhanced centering and holding power while providing ultra-smooth, consistent motion across the entire range. 
Built to handle the stress of high-load scenarios, the HS-5645MG includes a powerful cored motor and dual ball bearings for smooth operation and extended life. The durable metal gear train ensures you get consistent performance under load, making it ideal for 1/8 scale vehicles, aerobatic planes, and large-scale models. 
 NOTE: The HS-5645MG is the digital version of the HS-645MG . 
Key Features 
 High Torque Output: With a torque rating of up to 168 oz-in @ 6.0V, the HS-5645MG delivers exceptional strength for steering, throttle, and control surface applications. 
 Metal Gear Train: Built with 1 MP (Metal + Plastic) & 3 Metal Gears, it provides long-lasting performance and increased crash resistance over plastic gear counterparts. 
 Cored Metal Brush Motor: Designed with Hitec&rsquo;s cored motor, the HS-5645MG provides dependable speed, torque, and long service life. 
 Ball Bearing Support: Even during heavy-duty use, the dual ball bearings on the output shaft ensure smooth operation and reduced wear. 
 Standard Fit: Its standard size makes it compatible with many popular RC platforms. 
 Fully Programmable: Via the Hitec DPC-20 and/or HFP-30 digital servo programmers, the performance of the HS-5645MG can be fined tuned for customized response curves, fail-safe settings, and more. 
Specifications 
 Operating Voltage Range: 3.5V ~ 6.8V (2S LiFe) 
 Torque: 
4.8V: 143.0 oz-in (10.3 kgf-cm) 
6.0V: 168.0 oz-in (12.1 kgf-cm) 
 Speed: 
4.8V: 0.23 sec/60&deg; 
6.0V: 0.18 sec/60&deg; 
 Dimensions: 1.61 x 0.79 x 1.49 in (40.0 x 20.0 x 37.8mm) 
 Weight: 2.12 oz (60.0g) 
 Position Sensor Type: Digital 
 Output Spline: 24T
