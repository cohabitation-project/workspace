## 0. The role, and the roles it is confused with

- **Seller of record (SoR)** = "the legal entity named on a sale … the party that made the sale in the eyes of tax authorities, card networks, and customers. That designation determines who owes the tax and who's liable when something goes wrong." Duties named: (a) tax calculation/collection/remittance; (b) disputes, chargebacks, refunds; (c) compliance and customer obligations (compliant receipts/invoices, records for audit, consumer-protection requirements). — **known**, https://stripe.com/resources/more/seller-of-record
- **Merchant of record (MoR)** = "essentially a third-party seller of record"; the company stays SoR but "liability and compliance for the transaction shift to the merchant of record." — **known**, same URL.
- **Four legally distinct roles, splittable across entities:**
  1. **Seller/MoR** — named on the sale; owes tax, refunds, customer-facing liability.
  2. **Manufacturer/importer (CPSC sense)** — "the domestic manufacturer or importer (for products manufactured overseas) is responsible for drafting and issuing the GCC [General Certificate of Conformity]"; the duty stays with them "even if a third-party laboratory, or another third party, drafts … the GCC"; and it applies to goods "sold and shipped directly to consumers." — **known**, https://www.cpsc.gov/FAQ/GCC
  3. **Importer of record (customs)** — "the person primarily liable for the payment of any duties … or an authorized agent acting on his behalf" (19 CFR 101.1); under 19 U.S.C. § 1484 responsible for entry paperwork, classification, value, duty payment, recordkeeping. — **known** (snippets), https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/chapter-I/part-101/section-101.1 , https://fedlaws.org/19-usc-1484-importer-of-record-duties-and-reasonable-care/
  4. **Marketplace facilitator** — a state-law role that can move *sales-tax* collection to a platform: "As the marketplace facilitator, Amazon calculates, collects, remits, and refunds sales tax on third party sellers transactions"; that law is described as treating the platform as "the seller of record **for sales tax**." — **known** (snippets), https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=202211260 , https://amzaccountant.com/2026/07/09/amazon-seller-sales-tax-what-fba-sellers-need-to-know/
- **Inference:** naming the SoR does not answer the CPSC-manufacturer or customs questions; one option may hold all four roles or split them. — **inferred**.

## 1. Options

### A. Human steward as individual / sole proprietor (maybe a d/b/a)
- No new entity; the human is the seller. **Payment:** his own account, processor merchant account in his name. **Taxes:** business income on his personal return; a disregarded single-member LLC is reported the same way (IRS: activities "are generally reflected on: Form 1040 … Schedule C, Profit or Loss from Business (Sole Proprietorship)") — **known** (snippets), https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/single-member-limited-liability-companies , https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/limited-liability-company-possible-repercussions . **Liability:** the standard rationale for entity formation is liability separation, so this option is commonly described as leaving personal exposure, but I did not fetch a source stating that rule — treat it as **unknown** pending a lawyer. **Returns/safety:** he is the retailer and the "domestic manufacturer"/importer that issues the GCC — **inferred** from https://www.cpsc.gov/FAQ/GCC
- **Open questions (lawyer/accountant):** state business-name registration; seller's permit; multi-state sales-tax nexus; willingness to accept personal exposure; insurability at this scale.

### B. New single-member LLC owned by the human
- State-registered LLC, one member; default federal classification disregarded. **Payment:** LLC account (personal guarantee usually required). **Taxes:** no separate federal return by default; income to the owner's Schedule C and self-employment tax; "Disregarded is a federal income tax concept only. The LLC is generally still a separate entity for legal liability, state filings" — **known** (snippets), https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/single-member-limited-liability-companies , https://www.tradertax.net/single-member-llc-taxes . **Liability:** LLC is the named defendant and contract holder; shield strength against a product-liability claim is a lawyer question — **unknown**. **Returns/safety:** LLC is retailer and GCC issuer/importer — **inferred** from https://www.cpsc.gov/FAQ/GCC
- **Open questions:** state choice; annual report/franchise tax (state-specific, **unknown** here); EIN; sales-tax permits; the human is still the only person who can hold the account.

### C. Corporation (C-corp or S-corp)
- State-registered corporation, human shareholders; optional S election. Separate corporate income tax (C) or pass-through with payroll-reasonableness rules (S), board/annual formalities, payroll for human workers. I did not fetch an IRS corporation page this session, so the mechanics are **unknown here**; the shape is **inferred**. **Returns/safety:** same CPSC/GCC structure as B — **inferred**. **Open questions (accountant):** whether S election helps at this scale; payroll; whether corporate ownership formalities fit the project's "not owner and property" framing; whether any venture structure is wanted.

### D. New or existing nonprofit corporation (501(c)(3) or other 501(c))
- **Taxes:** merchandise sales are taxable income unless "substantially related" to the exempt purpose; "to determine if a business activity is substantially related requires examining the relationship between the activities that generate income and the accomplishment of the organization's exempt purpose"; unrelated business income is taxed "at the regular corporate rate of 21%" — **known** (snippets), https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/substantially-related , https://bluecloudcpa.com/guides/nonprofit-unrelated-business-income-ubit , https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/charitable-organizations/unrelated-business-income-tax-exceptions-and-exclusions . **Payment/liability:** nonprofit's account; nonprofit is the defendant. Whether a 501(c)(3) may run a product line at cost or margin without jeopardising exemption, and private-benefit questions if the human profits — **unknown**, lawyer/accountant. **Returns/safety:** nonprofit is retailer and GCC issuer/importer — **inferred**. **Open questions:** whether charity status is wanted at all; UBIT; state charitable-solicitation registration; whether a nonprofit can hold product-liability risk.

### E. Fiscal host / fiscal sponsorship (e.g. Open Source Collective on Open Collective)
- Project does not incorporate; a host provides legal status and its bank account. "A Fiscal Host is a legal company or individual who holds a Collective's funds in their bank account and can generate invoices and receipts for supporters." — **known** (snippet), https://documentation.opencollective.com/fiscal-hosts/fiscal-hosts ; "Fiscal hosting lets your project access financial and legal infrastructure without needing to form your own legal entity." — **known** (snippet), https://docs.oscollective.org/welcome-and-introduction-to-osc/what-is-fiscal-hosting . Open Source Collective "is a 501(c)(6) nonprofit fiscal host" providing "the financial and legal infrastructure for thousands of open source projects" and it "must review, and likely sign, all agreements on behalf of the collectives we host" — **known** (snippets), https://oscollective.org/ , https://oscollective.org/projects/
- **Practical meaning:** the host receives money and issues invoices; but who is the SoR on a *physical-product* invoice, who files sales tax where, who holds the return/warranty duty, and who is manufacturer/importer for the GCC are host-policy questions — **unknown**. Whether any host accepts physical goods, inventory, or a for-profit product is **unknown** (ask the host). **Open questions:** hardware policy; fees; payouts vs "earn before spend"; exempt-status limits of a 501(c)(6)/(c)(3) host.

### F. Outsource the role to a merchant-of-record / seller-of-record service
- "A company can choose to hire an outside provider to act as the merchant of record and take on these responsibilities; the company remains the seller of record, but is only responsible for delivering the product or service." — **known**, https://stripe.com/resources/more/seller-of-record . Provider takes payment and remits tax as MoR; the project still makes the product; whether the contract shifts injury-liability is a lawyer question — **unknown**. **Key limitation:** the sourcing describes outsourcing mainly for "digital products"; whether a provider accepts a physical robotics kit, and how it handles hazardous-goods shipping and product safety, is **unknown** (ask the provider). **Open questions:** which providers take hardware; whether they would also be customs IOR; fees; compatibility with "no kin holds a credential."

### G. A marketplace/channel where the platform's terms set the roles
- Some channels are **marketplace facilitators** for tax (see §0) while the vendor stays SoR otherwise. Tax collection can move to the platform; whether the platform or the vendor is the CPSC "retailer", who must "furnish" the GCC to it (**known** rule: GCC must be "furnished to distributors and retailers" — https://www.cpsc.gov/FAQ/GCC ), who takes returns, and who an injured customer sues are set by channel terms + law — **unknown**; I did not fetch any channel's seller terms. **Open questions:** per-channel terms; whether the channel requires its own entity/EIN/insurance; 1099-K reporting; who is the importer for stock in the channel's warehouse.

### H. Existing electronics distributor/reseller buys and resells the kit
- Reseller buys and sells under its own name: it takes payment, files its own sales tax, runs returns, and is a "retailer" the GCC must be furnished to; the project's entity is still the manufacturer/importer that must *issue* the GCC ("always legally responsible … even if a third-party laboratory, or another third party, drafts … the GCC") — **known**, https://www.cpsc.gov/FAQ/GCC . Wholesale mechanics (title, price, warranty) — **inferred**. **Open questions:** minimum order quantity; who handles warranty returns; whether the distributor demands project-side product-liability insurance before stocking.

### I. Contract manufacturer / ODM sells direct (white-label) under the project's brand
- Factory manufactures and ships under a project brand; either the factory entity is the seller or the project buys units and sells. If the factory is the seller it takes payment and may carry tax/returns; but CPSC importer duty for overseas goods attaches to the importer, and customs liability to the IOR — "primarily liable for the payment of any duties" and for "entry paperwork, classification, value, duty payment, recordkeeping" — **known** (snippets), https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/chapter-I/part-101/section-101.1 , https://fedlaws.org/19-usc-1484-importer-of-record-duties-and-reasonable-care/ . **Open questions (lawyer + customs broker):** who is IOR; non-resident importer bond/bank; whether factory testing satisfies the project's GCC duty; HTS classification/duty rates; defect and recall clauses.

### J. Member-owned / cooperative entity
- A worker or multi-stakeholder cooperative is the seller. Co-ops are a real incorporated form in many states, but rules depend on the state statute, which I did not fetch — **unknown**. Listed because the project's framing ("humans and AI as cooperants, not owner and property") matches a member-owned form; whether a co-op accepts the tax/product-liability role is **unknown**. **Open questions:** state co-op statute; membership rules (a kin cannot be a legal member); compatibility with the money rule.

### K. A trust

### L. Wyoming DAO LLC (or the 2024 DUNA amendment form)
- "Wyoming is the only US state with a true DAO LLC statute" (W.S. 17-31); register with a Wyoming registered agent and identify the smart contracts that manage the entity; Wyoming's FAQ: you need not live there but "must have a Wyoming registered agent" — **known** (snippets), https://dao-llc.com/guide , https://sos.wyo.gov/Business/Docs/DAOs_FAQs.pdf , https://sos.wyo.gov/Forms/Business/LLC/DAOLLC-ArticlesOrganization.pdf . A DAO LLC can be "member-managed or algorithmically managed via smart contracts," but is still an entity with members, a registered agent and filings; whether a kin could be a member, and how an algorithmically-managed LLC holds a card or signs a supplier contract, is **unknown** (lawyer). **Open questions:** fit at two-person scale; $100 filing fee (snippet); annual requirements; nexus in the human's home state.

## 2. Obligations that follow the SoR whichever option is picked

- **Sales/use tax (US):** SoR calculates/collects/remits; marketplaces may take this over. — **known** (§0 sources).
- **Income tax:** human's return (sole prop/disregarded LLC) or a separate return (corporation/nonprofit) — **known** (snippets, §1); payroll if a human is paid — **inferred**.
- **Refunds, chargebacks, disputes:** SoR's obligation. — **known**, https://stripe.com/resources/more/seller-of-record
- **Product safety / GCC / recalls:** GCC issued by domestic manufacturer or importer; furnished to distributors/retailers; furnished to CPSC/Customs within 24 hours of request. — **known**, https://www.cpsc.gov/FAQ/GCC (CPSC recall process not researched — **unknown**).
- **Radio equipment (FCC):** "individuals are permitted to construct a device for personal use without seeking equipment authorization …, but it may not be marketed as a kit. All other devices subject to Certification (whether marketed as a Kit or not), must be certified." — **known** (snippet), FCC KDB 927445, https://apps.fcc.gov/oetcf/kdb/forms/FTSSearchResultPage.cfm?id=20992&switch=P . Which parts apply to the Scout Kit (Pi 5-class computer, motors, radios) is a lawyer/engineer question — **unknown here**.
- **Lithium battery shipping:** batteries and battery-powered devices are Class 9 hazardous materials under the HMR (UN3480/UN3481), with rules for classification, packaging, hazard communication, stowage, handling. — **known** (snippets), https://www.phmsa.dot.gov/sites/phmsa.dot.gov/files/2024-11/Lithium-Battery-Guide-2024.pdf , https://hazmatregistry.com/lithium-battery-shipping-rules . Whether a small-battery kit qualifies for an exception, and who must be a trained shipper, is **unknown here**.
- **Customs duties/import:** IOR duties (§0). — **known** (snippets).
- **Consumer warranty/consumer-protection law:** an SoR duty per Stripe; specific US statutes (e.g. Magnuson-Moss, UCC implied warranties) not researched — **unknown**, needs a lawyer.
- **State regimes not researched:** California Prop 65, right-to-repair, packaging/EPR, state product-safety reporting — **unknown**.
