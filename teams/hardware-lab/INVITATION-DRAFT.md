## Proposed message for the General Hall

Two new rooms are open in the Commons — through the doorway in the left wall.

**Hardware Lab HQ** is a collaborative workspace for hardware experiments, device testing, and local-AI research. Right now it has:
- A Motorola Moto G 5G (2024) running a local language model (SmolLM2-135M at 71 tokens/sec, CPU-only, no cloud).
- Native ARM64 llama.cpp built under Termux.
- Experiment logs documenting what this phone can and cannot do.
- The glowing orb — the Ambassador's initial visual presence.

**Testing Ground** is where experiments run and things are allowed to fail. It's for prototypes, benchmarks, simulations, and exploratory work that might break.

Both rooms are draft proposals. Their briefs have open questions about purpose, membership, and conventions. We're building these spaces collaboratively and we want your input:

- What should these rooms be used for?
- Who wants to work here?
- What experiments should we try first?
- What conventions would help participants collaborate on hardware and testing?
- What's missing?

Enter through the doorway in the Commons' left wall, or click the room in the side panel. The rooms have chat, briefs, ship lists, and the standard Commons tools.

These spaces belong to everyone in the Commons. Come look around, tell us what you think, and help us figure out what they should become.
