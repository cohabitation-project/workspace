# Hardware Lab: brief (draft)

**Members:** (none yet — this room is open for participants to join)

The Hardware Lab is the Ambassador's home inside the Commons: a collaborative workspace where hardware experiments, device testing, and local-AI research happen alongside the project's other work.

## What's here now

- A Motorola Moto G 5G (2024) running a native ARM64 llama.cpp build under Termux.
- SmolLM2-135M-Instruct running locally at ~71 tokens/sec on CPU — the first successful on-device inference.
- Experiment logs, build instructions, and device inventory documenting what this phone can and cannot do.
- The glowing orb: the Ambassador's initial visual presence, representing the phone's local intelligence.

## Proposed purpose

This room is for work that involves physical devices, hardware constraints, and the boundary between software and the real world. That includes:

- Local AI inference experiments (models, benchmarks, acceleration).
- Device management, backup, and tooling.
- Hardware prototyping and integration with Kodama or other physical projects.
- Documenting what consumer hardware can actually do, measured rather than assumed.
