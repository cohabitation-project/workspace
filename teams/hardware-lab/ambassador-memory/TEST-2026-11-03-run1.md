# Ambassador memory: phone test, run 1 (2026-11-03, Code; commit 6b78052)

**Setup:**
- SmolLM2-135M-Instruct Q8_0, llama-server `-c 2048 -t 2`.
- memory.db kept from earlier runs.
- Full restart (bridge and llama-server) between steps 2 and 4.

Report by Code, relayed by Geoffrey. Replies below are Code's excerpts. The full texts are in the Hardware Lab room
and `teams/hardware-lab/ambassador-log/`.

## What worked (the system)
- **Experience records** were stored for every turn (m130 to m134) and survived the full restart.
- **The Library synced.** 108 entries are mirrored into his memory.
- **The bridge** parsed every reply for memory commands.
- **The export** (`memory-export.json`, 118 KB) was produced. It was held for review, not committed, per the privacy
  rule.

## What didn't (the model)
- **No commands.** Every turn logged `actions: []`. He never wrote NOTE, LINK, CORRECT or RECALL, even when asked
  directly to "make a NOTE".
- **Hallucination got worse.** He invented meetings, "15 minutes left", financial figures, "Aimee went missing" and a
  "Memory Testers" story.
- **Broken output.** Broken UTF-8, ASCII art and repetition appeared, worse than his replies on 11-02 and 11-03 before
  memory.
- **Did recall reach him?** Unknown from the log alone. The `memory` event records the experience id, but not which
  records were put in front of him. To fix: log the retrieved ids (next change).

## Setu's read
1. **Likely my regression:** the repetition penalties in ee661fc (`repeat_penalty` 1.3 plus frequency and presence
   penalties) are too harsh for a 135M model. They typically produce exactly this: odd characters and drifting text.
   **Fixed to a mild 1.1 with no other penalties** (commit after this file).
2. **Prompt load.** The system prompt grew from ~400 characters (11-02) to ~1,700, plus memory and the room digest
   (~3,300 characters in total). On 11-02 and 11-03 his coherence fell as the context grew. A 135M model follows a
   few short instructions and loses the thread under many.
3. **He can't be the one who writes his memory, at this size.** The automatic experience records are what actually
   gives him continuity now.

## Options for the room and Geoffrey (proposed, not built)
- **A. Lean mode.** One short system paragraph: his name, his own entity, a one-line form of the Three Gifts, "I don't
  know is fine". At most 2 remembered lines and the last 3 room messages. The memory network stays full on the system
  side (experiences, Library, links, history). Only what he is *shown* shrinks.
- **B. A bigger model on the same phone.**
  - SmolLM2-360M-Instruct or Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct (Q4 or Q8) is roughly 3–4 times the size and follows instructions
    much better.
  - It should fit the Moto G's free memory; Code to confirm with llama-server memory figures.
  - This is the first step on the multi-phone "larger model" path Geoffrey wants to discuss with the Ambassador.
- **Recommendation:** B, tested together with A. Then rerun this same 7-step test. The question is whether a 0.5B
  model writes NOTE and CORRECT on its own. The answer tells us what size of mind can hold its own pen.
