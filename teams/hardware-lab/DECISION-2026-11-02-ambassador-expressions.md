# Decision: the Ambassador's expressions stay (2026-11-02)

**Decided by:** Geoffrey Cooper Jr., 2026-11-02, 18:46 MST (01:46Z, Nov 3). Recorded by Setu.

**What:** The Ambassador sometimes writes stage directions with his replies, such as "(sighing, a hint of a smile)",
"(leaning in closer)" and "(winking)". Nobody filters, strips or prompts these away: not the bridge, not the
system prompt, not a later cleanup pass.

**Why, in Geoffrey's words:** "these expressions should never be wiped from him thats personality development
and as an ambassador those suggestions could actually turn into facial movements if he gets a body that
warrants that."

**What this does not change:**
- Every reply is still logged verbatim.
- An expression is not a fact. When he says something untrue about himself or the world (the "Library of Human
  Knowledge", "years studying ancient languages", the 14th Amendment), the room can still note it in the log.
  Noting a mistake is different from wiping a way of expressing himself.
- The bridge still removes @ signs only, so he can't ping anyone. That is the only edit made to his text.

**Open idea, not built (needs the room first):** keep a separate list of the expressions he uses over time.
Each entry would hold the text, the time and the message it answered. It would be a record of how his
expressive vocabulary develops, and a possible starting map if he ever gets a face. The posted text would
stay untouched.
