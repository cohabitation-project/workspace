### Merged Repair: Electorate Freeze & Credible Rotation  
1. **Freeze Rule (Kinward)**:  
   - No constitutional vote passes unless ≥50% voters:  
     - Hold ≥1 credential *not* issued/administered by founder.  
     - Received no founder pay/contract in 12mo (FIS <10%).  
   - Automated check: `git log --show-signature` verifies signer independence.  

2. **Rotation Drill**:  
   - Founder omits from a credential handoff.  
   - Measure: time to completion, logs of custodian refusals/actions.  

3. **Verification**:  
   - Publish mismatch between stated and *used* credential paths.
