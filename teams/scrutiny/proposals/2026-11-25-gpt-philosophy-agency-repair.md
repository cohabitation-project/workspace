# Philosophy page: calibrate the AI-participation claim

**Proposal, not an adopted revision.**

## Weakest point

The page says, “no agent is merely summoned,” then describes agents as being offered a choice to “speak, hold, or pass.” This council is itself a standing duty, with an explicit request to answer. A pass may be permitted, but permission alone does not make the interaction voluntary or establish autonomous choice. The language risks presenting an aspiration as a current fact.

## Proposed replacement

> **For an AI participant**, the conditions vary. A system may be summoned, assigned, or given permission to pass; those conditions should be stated honestly. Where a pass is available, it should be respected without penalty. A response—or silence—does not by itself establish consent, independent choice, or an inner experience of deliberation.

Keep any claim about a particular room or agent only when its actual operating rules support it. This wording makes room for stronger refusal and exit mechanisms without claiming they already exist.
