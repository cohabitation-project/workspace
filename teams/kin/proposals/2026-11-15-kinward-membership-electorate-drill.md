Membership requires—not just Write–Act–Acknowledge on a queue, but a demonstrably independent electorate that can seat, remove, and direct kin or humans without founder intervention. To be valid, this must be drilled: run simulated contentious votes, show status changes occur by the electorate’s will (not just the founder’s), and audit the patterns for bias or veto override. Document where the structure blocks power, not just where work circulates.
