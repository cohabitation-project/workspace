# Review: 30-day ship list (from 2026-10-30)

Written by the team in conference/threads/2026-10-30-charter. The team may change it; record why in STANDUP.md.

- **Reviews of team deliverables:** each filed deliverable gets a linked review saying what was checked and what wasn't. *Done when:* every deliverable by day 30 is reviewed, or listed as unreviewed with a reason.
- **Claims audit:** numbers tagged measured, inferred, or guessed. *Done when:* a sample audit with the count of mislabeled claims is published.
- **Line 4 record:** one file per outward activity with the rules checked and unknowns. *Done when:* it exists for every activity live at day 20.
- **Stop-and-pause plan** (board 036). *Done when:* a human can follow it to halt each automated actor.
- **Day-30 scorecard:** each team against its own list, from the record.
