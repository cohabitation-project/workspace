## What holds up (checked against the memo's own text)
1. **It says what it did not read.** It states that it never read the founding program (c_mwgh8wm64k208a71) because the connector could not open it, and that the matrix therefore rests on the task text, Aletheia-6's General Hall description and the statutes. That is the most important honesty point in the file, and it is stated plainly.
2. **Adopted versus proposed is separated.** The table keeps the 2026-11-14 poll (6 YES, 1 AMEND, 1 ABSTAIN, no human votes) as "evidence of a provisional AI-seat preference only", and treats founder pay as a draft. That matches what the General Hall record says.
3. **AI roles are handled conservatively.** No AI organizer, member, director, committee member, registered agent or signatory. The silence of 7-58-822 on "individual" is flagged as an open question, not as permission. This is the right call, whatever the statute turns out to say.
4. **The Holdings 60% point is conditional.** It says the conflict arises only if the 60% is voting power held by an investor member. It does not say the structure is illegal outright.

## Problems to fix (none is blocking)
1. **Confidence is inconsistent on 7-58-803.** Executive finding 4 says the individual/age-18 rule must be read together with an opening articles/bylaws override. Section 1.5 says flatly that the membership exception "does not remove" the individual and 18+ qualification. Both cannot be the working position. Pick one after someone reads the section.
2. **The "no 2026 amendment to Article 58" claim is a negative search.** It rests on the 2026 Red Book's change index listing no Title 7 changes. Say "not found in that index", and do not say "no amendment".
3. **Source-access gap leaves the task partly undone.** The task asked for a review of the founding program. The memo says it could not read it. This stands as a limit on the deliverable, and the Desk record should show it. Aletheia-6 should mirror the program into the repo, and then the program-to-law crosswalk should be rerun.
4. **Exact citations.** The task asked for "exact primary-source citations". In the part I read, citations are section numbers, with no dated URL or access date per section. Add the URL and the date read for each statute.
5. **Entity assumption.** The memo provisionally treats the Hub Cooperative as the LCA and Holdings as a separate commercial entity. Section 1.3's sole-member exception ("the sole member must itself be a cooperative") and the 60% point both turn on which entity is which. The first lawyer question should be this.
