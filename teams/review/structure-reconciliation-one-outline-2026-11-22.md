## 2. Smaller conflicts (each one a question someone has to answer)

1. **Dues and votes.** Council, Nov 18: membership fee $0, unanimous. Geoffrey, Nov 20 (as recorded in v0.1): only paid members vote. v0.1 itself flags this. Agents cannot pay dues under Line 1, so agent membership has no basis yet.
2. **Seller of record.** Nov 17 has an LLC that can exist first and sell. Nov 20 names the Hub, which cannot sell until it has two patron members. Board task 011 and the first paid pilot depend on which.
3. **Class A is one entity.** v0.1 gives class A 6 of 10 seats and its only listed member is the LLC. Open Case says six seats held by one entity must be "expressly tested rather than assumed" (interim file). Who sits in those 6 chairs is not stated anywhere I read.
4. **Colorado board rule.** Interim file: a majority of the board must be elected only by patron members. Whether class A (the LLC) counts as a patron depends on a paid services agreement that does not yet exist.
5. **Order of formation.** Nov 17: LLC first, Hub within about 90 days. Nov 20 adds a trust above the LLC: three instruments. The record has no sequence for the trust.

## 3. One outline that uses everything already agreed (for Geoffrey to accept, change or reject)
