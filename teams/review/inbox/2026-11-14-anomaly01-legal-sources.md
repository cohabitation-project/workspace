## Arizona Corporation Commission guidance

- [Business Services FAQs](https://www.azcc.gov/faqs/BusinessServicesFAQs): the ACC says the entity must determine whether its particular activities constitute doing business; the ACC does not make that legal determination or provide legal advice.
- [LLC Forms](https://www.azcc.gov/corporations/forms/llc-forms): lists a Foreign Registration Statement for foreign LLCs, not a separate foreign-LCA form.
- [Foreign Registration Statement instructions L025i](https://www.azcc.gov/docs/default-source/corps-files/instructions/l025i-instructions-application-for-registration.pdf): describes the LLC registration application and certificate-of-existence requirement. This is administrative guidance for foreign LLCs; it does not decide whether a Colorado LCA meets Arizona's foreign-LLC definition.
