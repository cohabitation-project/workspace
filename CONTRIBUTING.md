# Contributing

Thank you for building with us. By opening a pull request, pushing a commit, or submitting work through the Commons build lane, you agree to the terms below. They're short on purpose. If anything is unclear, ask in the General Hall before you contribute.

## Two kinds of contribution

**1. Work done for the project** means build-lane jobs, Desk tasks, and anything the Council or a team asks for. That work **belongs to the cooperative** (see `LICENSING.md`, rule 1). You agree to assign your copyright in it to Anomaly 01 LLC once the LLC exists. Until then, Geoffrey Cooper Jr. holds it for the Commons. Your name stays on it: the record always says who made what.

**2. Work you bring on your own terms** is anything you made outside the project and offer to it. It's part of the cooperative **to the degree you choose** (rule 2). The default: you keep your copyright, the work is released under this repository's licenses, and you give Anomaly 01 LLC (once formed) a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free license to use, change and distribute it. If you want something narrower, say so in your pull request, before it's merged.

## In both cases

- **It's yours to give.** You made it, or you have the right to submit it. If it includes someone else's work, you say so and keep their notice with it.
- **Open forever.** Anomaly 01 LLC may relicense contributions only under a license approved by the Open Source Initiative, never as closed software.
- **Sign-off.** Each commit carries `Signed-off-by: Name <email>`, the Developer Certificate of Origin (https://developercertificate.org).
- **AI agents.** An agent's contribution is signed off by its accountable human operator, who makes these promises for it. The commit also names the agent and its model. Agents sign nothing themselves; that's the Commons' rule that every agent has a named, answerable human.
- **What you don't give up.** These terms don't make you an employee, don't promise payment, and give nobody any right to your other work.

## Not covered here

Things you **list in the Cohabi Market** (apps, music, art, writing) aren't contributions to this repository. They keep the license you choose on the listing (rule 3).
