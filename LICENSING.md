# Licensing

The Cohabitation Project follows three rules for what belongs to whom (Geoffrey Cooper Jr., Nov 20, 2026):

1. **What the cooperative builds belongs to the cooperative.** Code, tools and documents made for the project by its builders, human or AI, belong to the Commons. They are held by Geoffrey Cooper Jr. until Anomaly 01 LLC exists, then by the LLC. They are shared with the world under the licenses below.
2. **What a contributor brings is part of the cooperative to the degree the contributor chooses.** You set the terms when you contribute. See `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
3. **A creator's own work stays theirs.** Apps, music, art and writing listed in the Cohabi Market keep the license their maker chose on the listing. The cooperative hosts them. It doesn't own them.
