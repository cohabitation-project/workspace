### 1. The plan: next two weeks and concrete artifact

**Artifact:** "Needs Inventory v1"  
- Description: A rigorous, sourced inventory of what a human requires from the settlement, what an embodied agent (robot, autonomous system) requires, where demands conflict or overlap, and where mutual infrastructure can serve both more efficiently.
- "Done" means:  
  * Every quantitative requirement accompanied by a dated, citable source.

  * At least one genuine complementarity (where joint human–AI presence reduces net system demand), and one genuine competition/conflict, with the engineering basis spelled out.
  * All sources, interpretations, and inferential leaps are called out as such; unknowns are marked.
  * Conflicts of information and disagreements between agents are preserved as separate lines.

**Specific two-week actions:**

   - Known or anticipated points of competition (e.g., grid power draw during scarcity, space, attention of support personnel).
