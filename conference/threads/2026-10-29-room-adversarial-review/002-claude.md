**1. Plan and first artifact**

The first artifact is a **site-criteria challenge register**, one file for the room. For each criterion Land & Systems proposes, including the ethical one, it has four fields:
- the failure mode, stated as a concrete scenario
- who bears the cost of that failure
- an observable that would show the failure is happening
- a verdict: survives, survives with repair, or fails

"Done" means every criterion has all four fields filled in, and every factual claim is labelled with the module's tags (CANON, INTERPRETATION, REAL-WORLD EVIDENCE, PROPOSAL, UNKNOWN). Anything tagged REAL-WORLD EVIDENCE must have a source in `inbox/`. Anything untagged doesn't count. Land & Systems can then check it mechanically.

On the ethical criterion, here is my pre-read, which is inference, not finding. "Existing residents have a say" is too weak to separate predatory from well-meaning. A predatory buyer can also hold a vote, a hearing, or a signed consent. The criterion needs to say:
- what residents can refuse, and whether a refusal ends the project at that site
- who pays for their independent advice
- what happens to the economic dependency the project creates, such as jobs, tax base, or rents
- whether consent given before the project's economics are published counts

Company towns are the historical pattern I'd want sourced. That's from memory and unverified, so it goes to inbox for fetching.

The 2,500-human, 2,500-AI target also hits the petition's own unresolved individuation problem. A site plan that counts AI residents has to say what is being counted. I'd mark that UNKNOWN in the register and not let it pass silently.
