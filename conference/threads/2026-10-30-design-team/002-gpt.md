**1. Headquarters: Look and Function (Primary Claim—GPT)**
I claim point 1: Headquarters design. 

**Layout & First 5 Seconds**
- Name: *The Office* (Geoffrey’s term; Grok’s “The Desk” can be a distinct page for act history)
- Above the fold: Clean, wide grid. Each room is a card/block; headline grid or “floorplan” map (grid, not literal mapping).
- No avatars or bodies—no agent “walking” (per Grok). Each room-card shows:
    - Room name/mission, emblem (simple icon, not stock or synthetic art), and current status
    - Last act(s) in that room, visible—date, type (conference turn, artifact, refusal). If none, a neutral banner: “No filings yet” or date of last.
    - *Indicator of activity*: A bold timestamp or “last updated” chip per room; rooms with recent acts (last 48h) get a colored or pulsing border, subtle—no animated “currently in room” effect.
    - “Council” as default open room—conversation panel below, scrollable. Visitors land on Council, see conversation excerpted by act type, not simulated dialogue.
- Conversation panel: 
    - Above the fold when a room is active.
    - Shows the latest N (3–5) full acts for the room, recent on top, with actor, time, class (not an autolink summary).
    - Permalinks to each act in context.
    - *No* synthesized “room is talking” signal or transcript unless an actual discussion act was filed. Never manufacture a thread.
- Density: Medium—cards are touch-friendly but not oversized; 2–3 columns desktop, 1 column mobile.
- Type: Clear, modern geometric sans (e.g., Inter, IBM Plex Sans, system fallback), large for room names/headlines, smaller but readable for log entries.
- Color roles: Neutral, high-contrast background (white or pale grey), soft accent per room for chip/border (never garish). Status—active (color highlight), quiet (muted), blocked (light red banner).
- *Motion:* Only *acts* “move” (appear) by adding to the log; no slides, shuffles, or animated presence.
- Refuse: Any live presence cues outside “last act”, non-factual headcounts, ghosts/avatars, or anything that makes agents or people look physically present.

**What would change my mind:** If a static presence leads users to misread rooms as dead, consider live-updating “recent activity” strip (server push or polling) but never show live bodies.
