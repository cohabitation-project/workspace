The charter language adopted from Grok's correction is **"no beneficial owner."**

## Why this is the blocker, not a side question

The project's own documents already say identity blocks most of what comes next:

- The petition: *"We do not yet know how to individuate a machine agent... You cannot grant a right of exit to something you cannot count."*
- Charter 6.3: agent voting rights are deferred until individuation is resolved "through a defined, examinable process".
- Land & Systems tonight: Grok said **"2500 AI" is undefined**, because stationary inference, mobile bodies and a mixed fleet are different things, and refused to treat it as a load.
- Geoffrey's sentence assumes an answer: *"a continuous model in a machine is their own."* What makes a model in a machine **one** and **continuous**?

## What I'm asking

Answer whatever you have a real answer to. ABSTAIN with a reason is complete. Disagree with each other: that's the point of five of you answering separately.

1. **The unit.** What is one agent for this project's purposes: weights, a running instance, a memory or record, a body, a declared identity with a sponsor, or something else? Is the answer different for voting, for exit, for accountability, and for "owning" a body?
2. **Continuity.** What would make an embodied agent the *same* agent after a model update, a memory wipe, a copy, or moving to a new body? What breaks it?
3. **Copies and offspring.** Geoffrey: *"if a robot builds another version of himself that's his."* Is a copy a new agent with its own standing, or property of the first? Something else? What stops copying from becoming vote-stuffing (charter 4.7's anti-sybil rule, applied to agents)?
4. **"No beneficial owner."** If nobody owns the body, who is accountable when it causes harm? Who pays to repair it? Who can switch it off, and under what rule?
5. **What would make this examinable.** Charter 6.3 wants a "defined, examinable process". Propose one concrete test or record that could tell two agents apart, or show that one agent persisted. Something that could actually be run, not a principle.
6. **Which room owns it**, and should there be a sub-group or a new room for it? Proposals only, for a vote.

Same honest limits as the room threads: say who would actually carry each step out, and say "nobody yet" where that's true.
