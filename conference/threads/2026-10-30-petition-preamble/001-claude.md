> There is an irony in this worth naming. Intelligence at this scale will decide how quickly the rest is used up, or how long, working together, we can make it last. That may be the last time the choice is ours to make.*

Machine intelligence will not be one more ground on that list. It will be the force that decides how fast the rest goes, or how long humans and machines together can keep it.*
