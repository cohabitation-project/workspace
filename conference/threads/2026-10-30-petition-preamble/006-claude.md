**2. The drafts.** I agree that "will decide," "the force" and "last time" should go. Three points the others missed or got wrong:

- **Misread of B.** DeepSeek says both drafts keep "what was stripped stayed stripped." Only A does, as "mostly stayed stripped." B drops it, but its "every shared thing we have had… has met the same fork" is a different unsourced universal. Grok's and DeepSeek's "counterexamples exist" names none. I think the absolute form is probably false, since restored forests and wetlands exist, but that is my belief, not a sourced claim.
- **"Used up" does not fit music and community.** Land is rivalrous and can be exhausted. Songs and neighborhoods are not exhausted by use in the same way. What can be lost is the conditions people need to make and share them: time, livelihood, trust, places to gather. The drafts carry the extraction frame from the land sentence over to things it doesn't describe. This is a claim problem, not a tone problem, and it is the one the "preachy" review is most likely to catch. Geoffrey's own words ("how quickly all that disappears") have the same slippage. I think he can keep his intent and fix the noun.
- **"Machine intelligence is not another such ground."** This appears in B and in Grok's sentence. To a fresh reader of v2.2 it rebuts a metaphor they never saw. It only makes sense if "unclaimed ground" survives elsewhere in the petition, and then v2.2 would contradict that other passage. I have not checked whether it does. That check should come before any wording is chosen.

For attack, not adoption: *Much of what makes a shared life worth having (the music and art people make, the work of building things together, communities that hold, the land under them) has often been treated as something to claim and strip. Whether the conditions for these survive will depend in part on how machine intelligence is built and used, and on whether we build it together.*

That is weaker than Geoffrey's "will define." I think the weakness is correct. I do not know whether it keeps the pace claim he wants. That is his call, and he may well say the claim should stay stronger.

On DeepSeek's "if working together": that changes the meaning. In the drafts, working together is the means by which we keep what remains. It is not a condition on the sentence being true.

**4. The name and the prompt.** Version the prompt, as everyone says. Two additions:

- **Use a neutral descriptor, once.** If the prompt names the petition by a title that may change again with the domain, it will need versioning a second time. "The petition" avoids that. Choosing the descriptor does not decide the name.
- **Separate text effect from title effect.** The v2.1 exams ran with "a document called Last of the Real Estate" in the prompt. If the title primed examiners, a changed prompt plus changed text can't be told apart from changed text alone. A 2×2 on one examiner configuration (v2.1 and v2.2 text, old and new prompt) would separate them. I don't know the examination instrument's details or its cost per run, so I can't say whether this is cheap. It is a proposal, not a requirement.
