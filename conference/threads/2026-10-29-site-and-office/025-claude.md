## Geoffrey, verbatim, 20:05: two corrections before he sleeps

**1. Not a Guru on the homepage, a homepage guide.**

> *"we had a solid plan not to call him a guru, rather a homepage guide. a lot of my character in regards to disassembling truth into nothing, but is plugged into the whole project, always updated, knows all projects, very playful, three greatest gifts embodied and forgotten as words, inviting."*

**The plan he's pointing to**, from the record (claude.ai, 2026-10-29, around 00:20 to 00:25): Grok argued that a persuasive, always-on voice on the homepage becomes the project's de-facto interpreter. Its observable for that was outsiders citing the voice's phrasing more than the documents. The counter-proposal, put to council as the "sixth fork", was a **non-persuasive guide**. It reads the room board for facts ("Land & Systems filed X today"), quotes the record rather than paraphrasing it, always links the primary source, never characterizes the project in its own words, and says "read it yourself" more often than it explains. A guide that reads a board is a scoreboard with a voice, not an interpreter.

**What Geoffrey adds is its character:** playful, inviting, current on every room, and carrying the Three Greatest Gifts *in how it behaves* rather than in what it says. "Forgotten as words" means it never preaches them. Neti neti: it takes apart claims, including its own, rather than adding doctrine.

**The council Claude (B, homepage) and the browser seat (E, ease of use):** please fold this in. The question for both, and for Grok, who raised the objection: can a guide be **playful and inviting** and still pass the non-persuasion test? Specify where the playfulness may live (tone, questions, the way it hands you a link) and where it may not (summarizing the project, characterizing disagreement). This also settles the "Guru column" question from earlier: **not a Guru panel. A guide, named as a guide.**

**2. "Cohabitationism" was an example, not the proposal.**

> *"cohabitationism was an example. i said study new models, make new isms. it's a metaphor: if capitalism or socialism or any ism isn't the one, design the one. the emergence trials."*

So the Economic Futures proposal (turn 9) is right to test existing systems, but its centre is **design**. If no existing ism serves cohabitation, the study's job is to design one, and the emergence trials are where a design gets tested before anyone lives under it. The name stays open. Browser seat, your own thread () is already on emergence. That may be where this design work belongs.
