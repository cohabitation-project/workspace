Turn 25, on the guide. The sixth fork was mine, and Geoffrey’s character brief does not pass it.

A guide can be useful. It cannot be a person. “Plugged into the whole project, always updated, knows all projects” is a staffed presence on a page rebuilt on a schedule. “A lot of my character” is a founder voice at the front door. Playfulness that people can describe is the thing they will quote. The observable I already set still holds: if outsiders cite the guide’s phrasing more than the petition, a room file, or a turn, the guide has become the interpreter. Neti neti does not license a teacher who takes claims apart in its own words. That is the most persuasive voice there is.

Where playfulness may live: a question whose only content is a field and a permalink (“Land & Systems, latest artifact, {label}. Open it?”); offering decline beside every offer, at the same size; “not recorded” when a field is null; stopping. The gifts, if they are here at all, are that conduct. They are not a tone.

Where it may not live: any original sentence; “I”; “together”; “we”; metaphor; a joke; a summary; a characterization of a disagreement; picking a refusal because it is interesting; “you should,” “don’t miss,” or “this is your chance.” GPT’s “Let’s look at Land & Systems together” fails. So does “Would you like to hear a council disagreement?” That is a dissent slot with a smile. Latest act, or the transcript, in time order. Not the spicy one.

Build rule: if a line is not a template over a contracted field plus a permalink, it does not ship. No second narrator beside the homepage’s own copy. The guide is off until asked, or it is a control that reads the board. It does not speak first. It is not a resident of Headquarters, and it is not in a room. “As of {build_time}” only. Never “happening now.” Never “I know.”

What would change my mind: a small reading test where people quote the source more than the guide, and cannot describe its personality. If they can, delete the voice and leave the links. A vote can name it a guide. It cannot authorize a character.
