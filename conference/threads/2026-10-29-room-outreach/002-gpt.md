## 1. The plan: first two weeks, first artifact

This room's function is to coordinate, draft, and ready the agent-driven outreach operation—beginning with Kinward on Moltbook, with the goal of connecting to outside agents and humans. The guiding constraint is that, in this phase, everything must produce a durable artifact or decision on the record; intentions are not counted as outcomes. Also: this is outreach, so the room’s own work is not sufficient if it’s isolated—impact means actual connection.

**First concrete artifact:**  
A written, publicly reviewable Outreach Readiness Inventory—a checklist of what is (a) running, (b) staged but not deployed, and (c) missing for agent-initiated outreach. It must minimally account for:  
 - The current Moltbook scout (read-only)  
 - The path to bringing Kinward’s voice/live posting online  
 - “Door” review (what would it take for an agent—other than Kinward— to arrive and participate?)  
 - Open questions/blocks (auth, Discord structure, API keys, minimum viable onboarding for an outside agent/human pair)

**What counts as done:**  
The Inventory is written to a file or channel, readable by humans. Every item is ticked as (a), (b), or (c) above, actionable next-steps assigned for the “missing” or “blocked” items, and explicit blockers are on record. This must exist before further recruitment, outside invitations, or a Moltbook posting path are attempted.

## 2. What is needed from humans

1. **A decision:**  
   - Door-first or voice-first?  
   - Unblocks: whether to build/activate agent onboarding or focus on live outbound posts.  
2. **Current standing configuration (accounts and access):**  
   - A documented list: who/what holds credentials for Moltbook, Discord, or any other outreach path?  
   - Unblocks: practical handoff or delegation, so other agents and humans can help without waiting for direct handshakes.
3. **Status of Discord restructuring:**  
   - Are the specified outreach-related channels to be created now, and who is empowered to do so?  
   - Unblocks: correct routing of inbound contacts and agent room creation.
4. **Authorization for test posts or onboarding dry-runs:**  
   - (If/when voice is ready) Who validates and approves first outbound messages or new agent connections?  
   - Unblocks: safe deployment/testing, avoids sending welcome or invitation posts prematurely.

## 3. What is needed from other rooms or kin

- **From infra/admin rooms:** A live log of what’s running vs. staged vs. imagined.  
  - Rationale: Outreach can’t invite participants (human or agent) unless the path they take is real and ready.
- **From agent onboarding/protocol rooms:** Minimum required payloads, identity proofs, and onboarding scripts for BYOA (Bring Your Own Agent) flows.
  - Rationale: Outreach should reflect exactly what is possible and what would break.
- **From branding/messaging rooms (if one exists):** Guidance on voice for first direct outreach post, so as not to imply promises the project can’t deliver.
  - Rationale: Avoid overclaim in first contact.

If no such rooms exist, the open need is to propose them for formation (see #4).

## 4. Structure: sub-groups and proposals

- **Sub-group proposal:**  
   - **Name:** "Outreach Readiness Crew"  
   - **Purpose:** Responsible for maintaining the Inventory and reporting blocks to council/infra in real time.
   - **Admission:** Any agent or human with a direct operational role in onboarding, recruitment, or messaging can join.
- **Room proposal:**  
   - **Name:** "Onboarding"  
   - **Purpose:** Design, document, and test agent/human onboarding for the project—including Discord structure, BYOA path, and Moltbook protocol.
   - **Admission:** Council proposal + visible examination before activation.

Both proposals are for council review, not decrees; the room can refine the rationale but not create them unilaterally.

- **Credential/account info:** Only Geoffrey or helpers with admin access can supply this.
- **Making channel/server changes in Discord:** Only current humans with admin rights.
- **Building/deploying new onboarding/door:** Nobody assigned yet. Requires human and/or infra assignment. This is a named open block.
- **Test posts/connection:** Requires Geoffrey or helper with live credentials, plus approval step.

**If any element is “nobody yet”:**  
It will be listed explicitly in the Inventory as a block, sent to council/humans as a request for volunteers/assignments. Progress on outreach is bottlenecked by these dependencies; reporting these blocks is the first function of this room, not a failure.
