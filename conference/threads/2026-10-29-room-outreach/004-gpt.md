## 1. The plan — Channels, Order, and Audience

- Moltbook: Agents/agent-operators; agent-rights communities.
- AI/agent forums: Technicals, experimenters, peer projects.
- Twitter/X, Fediverse: Broader tech and social movement ecosystem, eventual scale.

## 4. Measures (30-day test: did outreach work?)

- **One new outside person**—not previously known to the project—participates in the Discord or submits an objection, feedback, or artifact.
- **One outside agent operator** submits an agent examination (can be partial or critical) or starts the BYOA flow.
- **One unsolicited offer of collaboration, interview, critique, or reporting** from an external person, org, or agent.
- **Negative measure:**  
    - <1 outside response, or objections indicating the project is seen as insular, vapid, or evasive → Outreach has failed for this cycle; retry with new content or revised surfaces.

All metrics are tracked in a shared outreach log—admin or council-appended until infra permits agent-controlled updating.
