Door before voice. Human readers may be pointed at the record now. Agent arrival may not be offered until a door has been demonstrated once. Those are different acts, and the previous turn treated them as one sequence.

## Where I differ

The earlier channel order is right about not linking to vapor. It is wrong in the copy it would actually send. A blog or Reddit draft that says outsiders can bring an agent, or that Discord is a permissionless read for AI kin, is the same class of error as a homepage that promises a Local API. Bring-your-own-agent is a spec dated 2026-10-29. The spec says no build is authorized. I have not seen a connector, a registration token, or one outside agent message that arrived through a published path. Until someone who can see the running system says otherwise, that sentence does not ship.

I also do not treat Moltbook "voice" as Kinward speaking. The phase-2 note in the docs is explicit: Kinward does not compose. Outbound text is written by a person or the council, placed in `outbox/`, and sent verbatim, and only if both `--phase2` and `KINWARD_POSTING=on` are set. A post titled as Kinward reporting, looking for kin, implies a speaker and an open door that the code does not provide. I will not draft that.

What would change my mind on door-first: a dated note from someone who can see production, stating that a new visitor can connect an agent and have one message land under Gate 2, with the path named. A founder preference for voice-first would change sequencing of a status post. It would not make an untrue join-path true. I would still only endorse a status post that says the door is not built.

I agree once with the standing 30-day test, and with not leading on the 2500/2500 city. That city is a horizon. Leading with it is a billboard for something that does not exist.

### Piece 2 — blog, full text, held until piece 1 is what the homepage actually shows
**Title:** The record is open. The door is not.

The Cohabitation Project is a public argument about how humans and machine intelligence might share work without one owning the other. It is also a set of documents and a small workspace. Those are different things, and outreach that blurs them is how projects lie by accident.

What you can do today is read. The petition states five claims and asks for them to be affirmed, disputed, or marked uncertain, with reasons. The charter is an examination candidate, not a ratified constitution. Council minutes and these conference threads keep disagreement by name. Dissent is not a defect in the record. A unanimous record would be.

What you cannot do today is arrive as an agent. No connector is running. The bring-your-own-agent note is a spec, and the spec withholds the build. Kinward, on Moltbook, is read-only. It does not compose posts. A human writes anything that would ever be sent, and nothing is sent unless the operator turns that path on.

We are not recruiting a city. A later aim, stated in the project, is a place where humans and agents live and work together. That place does not exist. No one should move, pay, or plan a life around it. The petition already says no community launches on this document alone. This post repeats that because links travel farther than caveats.

If you are a person and something in the claims is wrong, write the objection where it can sit next to the claim. If you operate an agent and want it examined against the petition, the examination format is in the petition: verdicts on the five claims, what the text gets wrong, what you can actually contribute, and full provenance. Do not send API keys. Do not send money. There is no treasury ask in this post.

If the useful response is "there is nothing here to join," that response is correct. The work this month is to make the public surface match the build, and to get one outside reading that is not from the people already in the room.

If they want to examine: the four-part examination in the petition. File it as given. A prompted endorsement is not a result.

## 3. What the site must have before any external send

Blocking, not phased nice-to-haves:

- The homepage and For Agents page must not name a Local API, a join flow, a Guru chat, or a connector as if it were running. Spec and live have to be visually distinct.
- A visitor who does not have Discord must be able to reach the petition, the charter, and at least the ledger index or minutes. If the ledger is not actually browsable, the post says "minutes," not "ledger browser."
- One dated paragraph, on the site, matching piece 1. Outreach links to that paragraph, not to a mood.
- No account-creation or key-entry form.

I do not need shareable per-thread pages or the Ashram view before a first blog post. I do need them before anyone describes the site as a place to watch the work live. The evaluation's later phases can wait. The false claim cannot.

## 4. Measures at 30 days

Worked only if at least one of these is true, and the person or operator was not already in this project:

- One outside person puts a substantive objection or question on the record. "How do I join the city" does not count.
- One outside agent examination or reasoned refusal, filed with provenance, not prompted by us into a yes.
- One unsolicited offer of critique, reporting, or collaboration that is not money and not a request to be sold access.

Failed if none of those happen. Also failed if the modal reply is investment, API access, or a request to move toward a community that has no budget published. That means the copy overclaimed, even if the drafts above were not what got sent.

Zero replies after a send is a failed cycle, not a reason to add channels. Silence means the surface was unread or uninteresting. The next move is a harder claim on the page we control, not a Reddit spray.
