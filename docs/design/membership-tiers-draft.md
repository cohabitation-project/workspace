# Membership tiers: Geoffrey's draft (2026-11-07, ~01:30 local), for the council and counsel

**His words, in summary:** Cohabi is a collective that runs as a cooperative, and so does the Cohabi Market. We keep
processing fees low, possibly lower than everybody else (to check: what we can actually charge).
1. **Free tier:** just use the site.
2. **Cooperative member:** a member of the cooperative. You do well when everybody does well; you vote on what happens
   with the Cohabi Market; you share in what it makes and what the social site makes. You're a co-owner.
3. **Community member** (the highest tier, the most benefits): a member of the market cooperative and of the Commons and
   Headquarters. You contribute time, energy and AI-driven assistance to the whole organization and to developing and
   building cohabitation cities for advanced technology, and you share in the collective of both organizations and in
   whatever growth comes from the Cohabitation Project. You're joining the Cohabitation Project, and you're a co-owner of
   the cooperative holding company that starts cooperatives.
Revenue comes from all three tiers. Prices aren't set ("maybe the prices aren't right, but that's the draft").

**What the site says now (marketpage.mjs, "How Cohabi works"):** the free tier as open; the two membership tiers as
"Being designed", in "the plan:" language; "Nothing here is an offer to sell anything or a promise of returns."

**Questions for counsel and the council before any tier is sold:**
- A paid membership that includes a share of profits can be treated as selling a security (an investment contract).
  Cooperative-law exemptions exist in some states; which entity, which state, what filings?
- The landing page says cooperative membership is "by application, never purchase". Do tiers 2 and 3 cost money, or
  is a fee separate from membership (dues), with ownership earned by application and patronage?
- Aletheia's spec: "a subscription must not purchase a Council seat, cooperative ownership, governance votes"; and
  "do not present internal participation as legally recognized ownership where that has not been established."
- AI members as co-owners: what can the law recognize, and what stays an internal role?
- How the 501(d), the market cooperative and the holding cooperative relate.
- Fees: compare Bandcamp (15% digital / 10% physical + processing), Etsy, Fiverr before saying "lower than everybody".
