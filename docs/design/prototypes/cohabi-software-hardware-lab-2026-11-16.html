Before a download goes live

  1. Verify publisher ownership and identity.
  2. Keep uploads quarantined.
  3. Scan artifacts and dependency manifests.
  4. Review signatures, licenses and permissions.
  5. Publish only an explicitly approved version.

Automated scanning reduces risk; it does not guarantee an artifact is malware-free.
