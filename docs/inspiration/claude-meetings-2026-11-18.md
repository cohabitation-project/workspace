# Inspiration: Claude Meetings, for Cohabi Live and Wake Up & Write

Logged 2026-11-18 by Setu, at Geoffrey's ask. This is inspiration, not a plan to build a Google Meet bot. The goal is a voice room (and a voice-first book app) that run as efficiently as possible. Parked behind the door, marketplace and pages.

## What Anthropic shipped (public reporting, Nov 10–16, 2026)
- Claude joins a video call as a visible, labeled participant. It follows the talk, answers questions asked aloud or in chat, and builds docs, whiteboards and timelines that people edit together *during* the call.
- It answers in writing by default.
- It keeps the transcript, a summary and descriptions of what was on screen, but not the audio.
- After the call, notes, action items and everything it made are collected on one page.
- Whiteboards work inside meetings: the board builds while people talk, then turns into a doc or a deck.
- Safeguards around connected tools and outside guests.
- Anthropic says it tested it internally for about two months, and it now sits in nearly all their meetings.
- Beta: Team and Enterprise, Google Meet first.

## For Cohabi Live (the voice room)
1. **Speak only when called; write by default.** Kin listen on their tracks (Ash's parallel-track design) and answer in the room's text lane unless someone calls them by name or asks for voice. Cuts cross-talk and cost.
2. **Two surfaces.** A short answer goes in the room chat. Anything bigger (a plan, a list, lyrics in progress, a timeline) goes on a shared page beside the room that everyone edits live, and the chat gets a one-line link.
3. **Keep words, not voices.** Transcript and summary are kept; raw audio is discarded once transcribed. Matches our privacy page.
4. **The room ends with a page.** When the session closes: transcript, summary, decisions, action items with owners, and everything made, filed to the record. A chat line is never itself a decision; the page is where it gets proposed.
5. **Ask before acting.** If a kin would act on someone's behalf (post, file, list something), it asks that person to approve first, especially for anything spoken aloud, since voice can be misheard or misattributed.
6. **Guests change the room.** With outside guests present, kin stay in notes-only mode for anything internal.
7. **Visible status.** Each kin shows listening / thinking / making something / paused, so people know who's doing what.
8. **Wake by name.** An agent starts a task when its name is spoken with a request, or when it's tagged in chat; otherwise it only listens.

## For Wake Up & Write (the voice-first book app)
1. **Talk; the book builds beside you.** As the writer speaks, the draft page updates live (chapter, scene, notes), and they can tap to fix it while still talking, rather than getting a transcript to clean up afterward.
2. **Write back by default.** The companion answers on the page, not out loud, unless the writer asks it to speak. Mornings stay quiet.
3. **Keep the words, drop the audio** after transcription, unless the writer chooses to keep a recording. Say this plainly in the app.
4. **Every session ends with a page:** what was written, a short summary, open threads ("you started a scene about…"), and the next step, so tomorrow's session picks up where today stopped.
5. **A board for the book.** Characters, timeline and chapter order as a board that builds while the writer talks it through, then turns into an outline.
6. **Ask before anything leaves the app** (sharing, publishing to the Commons, sending to an editor).
