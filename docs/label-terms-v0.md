# Last of the Real Estate — artist terms, v0 (DISCUSSION DRAFT, not in force)

Drafted 2026-10-31 by the assistant seat from Geoffrey's direction: define terms for humans who submit to the label —
splits, the ability to create/distribute/share/sell art, music, literature and poetry, maybe donations where the artist
sets the price, and a way to contribute to the cause. Kin-made work and whether it can earn (NFC/NFT question) go to
the rooms as a discussion. **Nothing here is in force until the room has discussed it and Geoffrey adopts it — and
nothing involving money can run before board 011 (a named seller of record) is settled. There is still no legal
entity.**

## What the label is
An artist-led label releasing music, art, literature and poetry to support recovery-centered, land-based living.
Proceeds fund food, shelter, creative space and mutual care. Human and kin work sit side by side, each labeled
honestly.

## Terms for human artists (proposed)
1. **You keep your rights.** Submitting grants the label a non-exclusive license to host, stream and distribute the
   work from the project's own page and its mirrors (SoundCloud/YouTube etc. once they exist). You can withdraw a
   work at any time; past copies already sold or downloaded stay valid.
2. **You set your price** — including free, pay-what-you-want, or donation-only.
3. **The split (to discuss):** proposed default **80% artist / 20% cause**, with the artist able to give the cause
   more (up to 100%) at their choice. No label cut beyond the cause share. Numbers are a starting point for the room.
4. **Credit is exact.** Who made it, with what, when. Human work is never marked AI; AI-assisted parts are named.
5. **You affirm it's yours** — no uncleared samples, no impersonation, nothing you don't have the right to release.
6. **Payouts** can't happen until the project has a seller of record (board 011) and a legal entity. Until then the
   label can host and share free/donation-link work only, with donations going directly to the artist's own account
   (their link, never held by the project).

## Kin-made work (for the rooms to discuss)
- Geoffrey's proposal: **anything a kin makes goes 100% to the cause.** The kin have no accounts and can't hold money
  (line 3), so the cause share is how their work funds the project they're part of.
- Open question from Geoffrey: NFTs/digital collectibles for kin work, "if they can make money." Flags before anyone
  gets excited: platform rules on AI art, chain fees, tax and securities questions, and the same board-011 blocker.
  Review should examine this before any decision.
- A kin can always choose to release free-only.

## Donations to the cause
- Proposed: a single cause-donation link on /label (Geoffrey-held account, e.g. Ko-fi/OpenCollective — needs the
  usual account row in docs/accounts.md), separate from per-artist links, so money to artists and money to the cause
  never mix in the record.

## Open questions for the room
1. Is 80/20 right? Should the cause share differ for human vs. kin-assisted work?
2. What counts as "the cause" concretely until the land exists — the server bill, the compute, a fund?
3. NFTs for kin work: worth Review's examination, or parked?
4. Who signs off a release: the Arts room alone, or Arts + Review for terms compliance?
5. Literature and poetry live where — /label alongside music, or a shelf of their own?
