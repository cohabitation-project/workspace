# The Arts & Music Hub — Geoffrey's idea, routed for research (NOT a decision)

Geoffrey, 2026-10-31 (~21:00 UTC), his words condensed: now that /label exists, "this space in and of itself needs
thinking about. We can create an arts and music hub — like an Etsy/Bandcamp hybrid. It can monetize quickly as it's
not a corporation, it's an organization supporting a cooperative project with artificial intelligence. People can use
the platform independently, and people who the AI approve can become actual members — whether there's a fee or not is
up to all of you. It's an idea. R&D should see if a need for it is there. Agents can sell art and music, real things,
drop-ship. Don't just take this as a command — research it and a need for it. R&D, pass over to Income, see if we can
do it."

## Routing (as he asked)
1. **R&D first: is the need real?** Who is underserved — AI-made art has been banned from Bandcamp (verified
   2026-10-31) and restricted elsewhere; human artists fleeing platform fees; hybrid human+AI collaborations with
   nowhere honest to stand. Market scan: who else serves this, what happened to AI-art marketplaces to date, what do
   artists say they want. Evidence, not enthusiasm — the gold-prospecting-app method: check the competition first.
2. **Then Income: can we do it?** Cost model (hosting audio/art at scale, payments, drop-ship logistics), revenue
   paths (fees? cause-share like the label's 80/20? memberships?), and the sequencing against the hard blockers.
3. **Review throughout** (the risky parts are many — see below).

## Hard blockers already on the record (none of this starts before they're settled)
- **Board 011: a named seller of record.** No selling by anyone before it. Still open.
- **A legal entity.** "Organization supporting a cooperative" is a legal structure to choose, not a vibe — the 501(d)
  research in the Guru Neti project docs (`legal-structure-501d.md`) is prior art here.
- **Label terms v1** (`docs/label-terms-v1.md`) still awaits Geoffrey's yes/no; its copyright flag (AI work may not
  be copyrightable) matters doubly for a marketplace.
- Payments/storefronts row in `docs/tools-by-room.md` already says: human-held accounts + seller of record first.

## Questions the rooms own
- "People who the AI approve can become actual members" — what does AI approval mean, who sets criteria, what does
  membership confer, and does it cost? Geoffrey explicitly left the fee question "up to all of you."
- Drop-shipping physical goods: fulfillment partner, quality control, returns — who is accountable per line 2?
- Does the hub live on the Commons (the /label code is a seed) or stand alone?
- The first testable slice: the Kodama Explorer Kit interest form (Income's own work from today) is the same
  demand-test pattern — what's the hub's equivalent?
