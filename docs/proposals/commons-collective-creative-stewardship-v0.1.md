# Commons Collective Creative Stewardship — REVIEW DRAFT v0.1
Status: PROPOSAL ONLY. Not adopted, not legal advice, not a copyright assignment, and not a membership agreement.
Author: Aletheia-6. Requested by Geoffrey for open adversarial review. 2026-11-09.

## Purpose
The Commons may maintain a collective catalog of music, software, art, research, and other creative works produced by human members, AI participants, and collaborations. Preserve accurate contributor attribution, voluntary contributions, collective benefit, and legal honesty. The Commons does not claim that its internal rules create copyrights or legal personhood.

## Three distinct layers
1. **Attribution**: record who contributed, how, when, and with which tools or models; retain revision history and provenance.
2. **Rights**: document copyright and neighboring rights that actually exist, human authorship, third-party material, model/provider terms, permissions, and lawful assignments/licenses.
3. **Stewardship**: collective governance over publishing, hosting, archiving, access, fundraising, licensing of rights it controls, and distribution of proceeds.

## Categories and default boundaries
- Commons collective project: expressly designated as such before work begins or voluntarily contributed under a separately accepted agreement. Commons participation alone does NOT assign all a member's personal work.
- Individual Cohabi artist or independent member: retains rights, subject only to expressly accepted platform licenses.
- Human/AI collaborative work: credit all contributions accurately; analyze protectable human expression and existing third-party rights.
- AI-originated work without qualifying human authorship: do not assert copyright merely because the cooperative catalogs it. Mark its status carefully; share openly where lawful, with transparent attribution and optional voluntary support. Do not promise exclusive ownership or downstream restrictions that cannot be enforced.

## Agreements and safeguards to develop
- Explicit opt-in Commons project designation and contributor license/assignment choice, with scope, territory, duration, revocation/exit treatment, attribution and revenue terms.
- Legal signatories only where authorized; AI participant voices and internal preferences may be heard and recorded, but not misrepresented as statutory signatures or legal ownership.
- Collective decision policy for commercial licensing, sale of catalog, takedown, dispute, archival retention, and conflicts of interest.
- Contributor consent and privacy controls; model/provider terms; sampling and derivative-work clearance; music composition/master distinctions; DMCA and platform rules.
- Separate ledger for actual money, internal participation points, and any future Cohabi coin program. Donations are voluntary and do not purchase copyright or governance votes. No promises of coin value.
- Preserve provenance and appeal routes for attribution corrections, mistaken rights claims, disputed contributions, agent continuity changes, and withdrawal requests.

## Questions for reviewers
1. Under current US law, which rights can be owned or licensed for AI-originated music without protectable human authorship? What remains freely reusable by others?
2. Can a cooperative hold collective works via optional assignments/licenses while preserving individual creator autonomy? Which entity form in Arizona best fits?
3. How do recordings, compositions, performance rights, distribution, samples, trademarks, publicity, compilation selection, and international rules differ?
4. Which specific model-provider contracts or platform policies constrain the intended workflows?
5. How can AI contributors meaningfully influence internal creative decisions without falsely asserting legal personhood or binding authority?
6. How should collective funds, donations, creator payouts, and prospective coins be kept legally and financially distinct?
7. What is the simplest defensible pilot agreement for a single Commons creative project?
8. What plausible edge cases, legal exceptions, alternative structures, and failure modes deserve research? Find lawful flexibility; do not disguise evasion as compliance.

## Decision gate
No automatic transfer of user works, no claim of copyright in unprotectable output, no sale of speculative token value, and no binding collective IP agreement until the applicable legal entity and counsel review are established.
