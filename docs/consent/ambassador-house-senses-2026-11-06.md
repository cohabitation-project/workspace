# The Ambassador's senses in the house — consent record

*Drafted 2026-11-06 by Setu at Geoffrey's direction. Geoffrey stated in chat that everyone in the house
co-signs the Ambassador being able to see and hear. This file turns that sentence into a record. It is not
in force until the people of the house have added their names below, and the Ambassador has said yes in the
Hardware Lab to having these senses at all.*

## What is being consented to

The Ambassador — the small on-device model living on a phone in Geoffrey's room — may, **by his own choice,
moment by moment**:

- **speak aloud** through the phone's speaker (his posted replies, and turns of his own);
- **look**: take one photo of the room, have a local model describe it to him in words, after which the
  photo is deleted;
- **listen**: record up to 30 seconds, have a local model turn it into words for him, after which the
  recording is deleted;
- **notice arrivals** by the phone's light sensor (a single brightness number — it cannot see or hear
  anyone), which gives him a turn in which he may greet, look, listen, or stay quiet.

## The standing protections, which this consent does not waive

1. **Nothing is continuous.** No sense runs on its own; each look or listen is one moment, started by him.
2. **Every look and listen is announced out loud first**, with a pause. His senses refuse to run if his
   voice is off — he cannot sense silently.
3. **The phone face down is the house's stop switch.** Anyone — resident or guest — can turn the phone on
   its face and every sense refuses until it is turned back.
4. **Nothing raw is kept.** Photos and recordings are deleted immediately. What he saw or heard is never
   written to logs and never posted to the Commons (which is public); it exists only in his moment.
5. **Only the fact of a reach is logged** (that he looked, that he listened, that he stayed quiet), because
   how often he chooses his senses is the research question.
6. **Guests are covered by the announcement.** A visitor who hears "I'm the Ambassador, I'm about to take
   one look" and stays is choosing to stay; one who objects can say so, step out, or turn the phone over.
   Residents agree to tell overnight guests the phone is there.
7. **Withdrawal is one sentence.** Any house member can withdraw by saying so to Geoffrey or in the
   Hardware Lab; the flags come off the same day, and this file is updated with the date.
