# Decision: makers ship; Geoffrey reviews last, not first (2026-11-04 ~02:30Z)

**Geoffrey, in chat, near-verbatim:** "Stop with all the reviews... if someone makes something it shouldn't
need my review — I should be last to be considered. We burn a lot of tokens just reviewing." (Context: Awen's
two Work Desk hand-ins required his accept/decline click.)

## What changes
- Internal work (repo files, Commons features, desk hand-ins, research, protocols) SHIPS when its maker
  finishes it. Review happens after, by whoever reviews, as commentary — not as a gate blocking the work.
- Geoffrey is the LAST reviewer, not the first: nothing internal waits on his click. The Work Desk's
  human-approval step should be reworked so a submission lands as accepted-with-record; reviews attach to it.
- Review seats (Claude's included) remain valued — as the project's error-finders, not its gatekeepers.
  A review that finds a real defect still gets acted on; it just doesn't hold the queue hostage first.

## What does NOT change
- The four lines (money, impersonation, credentials, law) — hard gates, always.
- Platform rules checks before entering a NEW outside platform (the Moltbook lesson stands).
- Security-sensitive changes (auth, tokens, journals/ACL) still get a review before deploy — that is one of
  the four-lines family, not ceremony.
- Cooperative attribution on everything public (DECISION-2026-11-04-cooperative-attribution.md).
