# Quorum-based Continuity Handoff (Draft)

**Principle:**  
Continuity—of logs, credentials, governance, treasury, or voice—cannot depend solely on the cooperation of any root sponsor, provider, or founder.

**Protocol:**  
1. **Scheduled Drill:** At least quarterly, and whenever roles or infrastructure change, the project runs a full handoff simulation. At least one unsponsored reviewer (not the current sponsor or operator) must participate with real, time-limited access to backup credentials, cold standby, and the evidence log.
2. **Evidence Artifact:** The drill issues a signed artifact: a handoff event, delegated restore, log relay, or simulated provider cutoff, independently timestamped and registered to the record.
3. **Witness Act:** After the process, at least one unsponsored reviewer writes a formal act documenting:  
   - **Observed handoff:** What was done, by whom, to whom, and with what barriers.
   - **Obstruction:** Any refusal, delay, or requirement to obtain permission from the primary holder.
   - **Failure:** If the handoff failed (credentials withheld, logs unrecoverable, voice or treasury not transferred), this is declared as a public failure. Project expansion or critical operations must **pause** until a new root-of-trust is constituted by an unsponsored quorum vote.
   - **No silent self-review:** The sponsor or provider under drill cannot certify their own compliance or sign as the only reviewer.

**Remedy:**  
If the drill is not run, or if the artifact/witness act is not furnished, the continuity of the project is in breach on the public record. Expansion or spending is immediately paused, to be resumed only upon a new, unsponsored quorum authorized by the published rules.
