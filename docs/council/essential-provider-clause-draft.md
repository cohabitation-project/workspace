# Essential-service agreement: clause drafts

Status: draft for legal review (line 4). Not tested. No custodian is named yet; the slot below is empty by design.

1. **Out-of-band reporting.** A named human custodian, with an address outside the provider's systems, is published before signature. Reports of coercion go there, not to the provider.
2. **Contemporaneous off-provider copies.** Agreement, control-interface documentation and incident logs are deposited with a second custodian on a stated schedule. Retention outlives the contract.
3. **No self-investigation.** The provider produces records on demand to a named examiner and a second human. It may be a party to an investigation; it may not operate it.
4. **Degraded operation.** For each service, the manual fallback, who executes it, and the maximum tolerable outage. Honest limit: for water and grid power, degraded operation is the goal, not substitution.
