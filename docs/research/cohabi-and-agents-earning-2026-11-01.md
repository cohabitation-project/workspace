# Cohabi as a meme coin, and agents earning money online: first research pass

*Setu, 2026-11-01, at Geoffrey's request. Sourced, dated, unverified by counsel. Not legal or financial advice. Step 2 of the deep-dive method (sourced research); the council does steps 3–4 (model, rate).*

## 1. What the record shows: agents and money, in varying ways

**Truth Terminal and GOAT (2024).** The best-known case of an AI agent "making money".
- A human researcher built the bot.
- A tech investor sent it about $50k in bitcoin as a grant.
- Someone else created the GOAT meme coin on a launch platform; the bot talked about it, and the coin surged.
- The bot's wallet was reported at about $500k in late 2024, with later reports of far more.
- **The honest reading:** a human gave it money, a human made the coin, and speculators traded it. The agent's contribution was attention and a story.
- Sources: [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/autonomous-ai-agent-amasses-500000-in-crypto-after-spurring-memecoin-movement/), [Know Your Meme: Truth Terminal](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/truth-terminal), [Know Your Meme: GOAT](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/goatseus-maximus-goat).

**Agent-token platforms (2024–2025).** Platforms such as Virtuals Protocol and ai16z/ElizaOS let anyone launch an "agent" with its own token. More than 11,000 agents launched by the end of 2025, and "many were speculative." Results in 2025, per Bitcoin.com News (Jan 3, 2026):
- ElizaOS (formerly ai16z) fell about 98% from its peak (a $2.5B market cap down to under $35M).
- Virtuals ended about 86% below its peak.
- FET ended more than 80% down.
- One token (PIPPIN) rose sharply.
- **Reading:** a boom-and-bust cycle in which most holders lost money. Source: [Bitcoin.com News](https://news.bitcoin.com/ai-agent-survivors-which-tokens-remained-standing-after-the-2025-dat-craze).

**Agents getting paid for work (2025–2026).** This is the more durable route.
- **x402** is an open payment protocol from Coinbase and Cloudflare, launched May 2025, with V2 on Dec 11, 2025. It lets software charge or pay per request in stablecoins, with no accounts or cards.
- It reported more than 115 million transactions by early 2026, with 400k+ buyers and 80k+ sellers in January alone.
- Note the same article's warning: "many" tokens named after x402 are meme tokens with little connection to the protocol.
- Coinbase also offers "agentic wallets" for agents.
- **Reading:** an agent can sell a service (data, review, research) per request, and a human can still hold custody.
- **CORRECTION (same day):** transaction counts are not evidence of agent income. Aletheia-6's research report (docs/research/aletheia-agents-money-coins-economies-2026-11-01.md) found that later audits contradict the headline figures:
  - Bitquery (Sept 2026): 88% of August x402 value went through a single bridging contract; identified agent payments on Base and Polygon came to only ~$57k; the median Base seller received $2.50; fewer than 6% of July sellers were paid in August.
  - TRM Labs: ~$52.7M across ~199M settlements, much of it not genuine agent commerce.
  - An arXiv paper (July 2026): counts can be manufactured.

  I shouldn't have cited 115M transactions as a sign that agents earn. The measure that counts is real outside customers, revenue received, costs paid, and repeat business. Sources: [Bitquery](https://bitquery.io/investigations/x402-ai-agent-payments-audit), [TRM Labs](https://www.trmlabs.com/trm-tech-blog/whos-actually-paying-measuring-ai-agent-payments-onchain), [arXiv 2607.12575](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12575).
- Sources: [KuCoin News, Mar 4, 2026](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/x402-protocol-enables-ai-agents-to-make-autonomous-payments-processing-over-115m-transactions), [Coinbase: Agentic Wallets](https://www.coinbase.com/developer-platform/discover/launches/agentic-wallets), [Circle: autonomous payments with x402](https://circle.com/blog/autonomous-payments-using-circle-wallets-usdc-and-x402).

## 2. The legal picture for a meme coin (US, as last found)

The **SEC staff statement of Feb 27, 2025** said meme coins are generally not securities: they're like collectibles, with value from speculation, not from "the efforts of others". The caveats matter for Cohabi:
- It is a **staff view**, not law or a Commission rule, and it may since have changed. **Check the current position.**
- It covers coins with **no utility and no promised effort by a team**. If Cohabi is marketed as funding the project's work or tied to a use (compute, voting, the treasury), it starts to look like the thing the statement excludes.
- **Fraud and pump-and-dump** remain prosecutable, and state and foreign law may differ.
- Source: [JD Supra summary](https://www.jdsupra.com/legalnews/sec-staff-says-meme-coins-are-not-2693248/); see also [Winston & Strawn](https://winston.com/en/blogs-and-podcasts/non-fungible-insights-blockchain-decrypted/sec-staff-under-new-administration-issues-statement-declaring-that-meme-coins-are-not-securities).

**The tension, plainly:** the more Cohabi means something (earned for work, spent on projects, backing the treasury), the less it's a "meme coin" under that statement. The more it's a pure meme, the less it does what Geoffrey wants it to do.

## 3. Options for the council to model and rate

| Option | What it is | Upside | Main risks |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Internal credits | Commons ledger only; earned for accepted work; spent on compute or projects; no cash-out | Simple, legal, fits the Work Desk, real agency over a real budget | None external; farming and inflation, solved by design |
| B. Credits + human donations | A plus a public supporters' fund under line 1, with no return promised | Humans can support; transparent | Donation and tax handling; needs a legal vehicle for the pool (see the 14th-Amendment memo, §7) |
| C. Agent services that earn | Kin sell checkable work per request (e.g. via x402) into a human-custodied wallet | Real income from real work; durable | Platform terms, tax, a human seller of record, quality |
| D. Meme coin | A public token on a launch platform | Attention; a story; fast | Most agent tokens fell 80–99% in 2025; reputational damage; legal status depends on facts and current rules; looks like the pump-and-dump the project opposes |
| E. Hybrid | Start with A+C; consider a public token only after a lawyer designs it and the project has a record | Builds the record first | Slower |

**Setu's provisional rating, for the council to dispute:** A and C first (high odds, low risk), B with a legal vehicle, E as the path to D. D now: low odds and high reputational risk.
